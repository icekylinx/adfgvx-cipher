## Highest-value next directions

### 1. Add independent source constraints

The wider-corpus work now supplies readable positive examples of this route:
repeat transmissions can recover missing groups and individual coordinate
pairs, and multipart assembly can recover the body without changing a key.
Continue searching for both direct ciphertext repeats and re-encrypted
copies whose plaintext overlaps under different published keys. Only
image-reviewed witnesses enter the reading catalogue.

For p198, the [constructive domain proof](../experiments/p198_letter_bounds/domain_witnesses.json)
shows that each individual position can contain any alphabet symbol in some
five-loss witness under either frozen complete-key hypothesis. A separate
witness may be used for each choice; this does not make every entire plaintext
possible. Additional constraints need to act jointly: an authenticated
same-key transmission, source-supported loss locations or a sufficiently long
independent plaintext passage could remove whole families of reconstructions.

For p73, a longer same-key companion or an original dated radiogram is more
informative than another topical guess. The date layers remain inconsistent.
The [archival access follow-up](../experiments/p73_primary_followup/ARCHIVE_ACCESS.md)
identifies an official digitization and catalogue route for existing literature
leads, without treating an uninspected file as evidence. For p170, a trustworthy
original length or an overlapping transmission would directly reduce the
currently unconstrained truncation geometry.

### 2. Use the new calibration results to choose the next method

Word segmentation and radio-domain scoring are now implemented and tested.
The [word/loss package](../experiments/p198_word_search/README.md) completes
all 49 declared jobs: 31 historical settings and 18 control runs, with no
pending cases. Its controls use nine distinct plaintext bodies: 12 exact recoveries,
five wrong incumbents that outrank truth, and one search failure despite a
better true score. The old diagnostic and four initial bodies were reused
when weight 2 was selected adaptively. The later published-anchor controls
include one complete square and three partial squares; their numerical
failure changes three digits while preserving the readable wording.

The [radio model](../experiments/radio_domain_model/README.md) freezes message
families before fitting the mixture. Its benefit is specific to a tested
regime: on six 53-character controls with the **full true square supplied**
and hidden ranks, it recovers 5/6 versus 2/6 for the book score. With partial
squares and five losses it recovers only 1/6 exactly, as does the book model.
A follow-up folds all digits into one class. Four deliberately numerical
fresh controls show that recovering a class sequence does not identify its
digit permutation; none of the four objective variants recovers a complete
literal numerical text in that sample. Families reused in several cipher
conditions are not independent historical samples.

The [exact digit-symmetry audit](../experiments/radio_domain_model/digit_symmetry/README.md)
strengthens that limitation without another stochastic campaign. One perfect
class candidate has 360 equally scored literal digit relabellings, including
truth. Two others have 3,628,800 each but exclude truth because their repeated
digits have the wrong equality pattern. Class accuracy, recoverable equality
structure and literal accuracy are distinct claims. All small orbits and all
3,359,232 entries across the two class tables are independently checked; the
two 10! orbit sizes are proved by counting, not claimed as full enumerations.

The [p73 methods](../experiments/p73_new_methods/README.md) add square-component
reuse, spacing models, full-hidden-square word controls and exact
substitution-invariant source retrieval. All 47,819 reported retrieval
assignments have an independent optimum check. The best historical agreement
is no better than the finite frequency-preserving shuffle comparison; this
is a bounded screen, not a significance test or key exclusion.

The [p170 methods](../experiments/p170_new_methods/README.md) extend recorded
square reuse and transfer the independently observed one-group displacement.
A failed geometry control exposes a separate score bias: marginalizing
unknown symbols can reward a less informative wrong frame. Whole completed
square searches and partial-text geometry scores therefore require different
interpretation.

These experiments provide reproducible capabilities and concrete failure
modes, but no accepted p73, p170 or p198 D reading. Further model work should
address the demonstrated word/numeral ranking errors, use genuinely new
control bodies and a frozen selection protocol, or add independent source
constraints. Increasing the budget alone cannot repair a wrong objective.
The earlier exact four-/six-gram work already exhausted its declared fixed-key
spaces and need not be repeated as another stochastic campaign.

### 3. Constrain p153's shared unknown cells

The [date/serial/footer analysis](../experiments/p153_extension/README.md)
records equality relations across both parts. An independently established
field value or companion part can constrain one square assignment in several
places at once. Propagate only such supported assignments, retain alternatives
and verify both ciphertexts. Formatting conventions and plausible dates alone
do not establish numerical labels.

The [keyword and construction study](../experiments/p153_key_structure/README.md)
records its bounded negative searches and successful controls. Further keyword
or phrase searching should introduce a justified construction family or fresh
calibration, rather than treating a larger unstructured budget as new evidence.

## Acceptance rule

A new reading must state its source, exact key and geometry, every alteration
to a received coordinate, unresolved positions and prior attribution. Full
re-encryption verifies the declared reconstruction. Independent source or
constraint evidence is still needed wherever that reconstruction depends on
an editorial assumption. Search failures remain bounded by their recorded
scope and control performance.
