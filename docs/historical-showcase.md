## Historical connections

**An order and its amendment.** Joining the five-part shipping order recovers its own reference, `FR52751`, at the end. The later *Siegfried* telegram expressly amends that number and closes with `FR52787`. The sequence changes the destination from Konstanza to Odessa and is established by the references despite anomalies in the recorded header times. [The shipping study](historical-studies/black-sea-network.md).

**Money available in an account and money available to spend.** Instructions to stop procurement are followed by demands for pay, suitable denominations and local cash. The banknote, remittance and liquidation messages distinguish several operations needed to finance withdrawal. [The financial study](historical-studies/what-retreat-cost.md).

**Vehicles departing and personnel remaining.** Readiness orders, equipment requests and the later truck-departure report describe different stages of leaving. The report that the sender would stay with a number of men until Sunday places a limit on what the vehicle's departure meant. [The transport study](historical-studies/transport-and-demobilisation.md).

**Traffic continuing under changing authority.** The wireless records distinguish staff custody, station control, working links and permission to move. The Poestyen and Grosswardein texts extend that sequence through local disarmament conditions and the transfer of radio traffic. [The wireless study](historical-studies/voices-on-the-wireless.md).
