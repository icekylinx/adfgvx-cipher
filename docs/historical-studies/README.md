# History in the intercepted traffic

[ADFGVX Cipher](../../README.md) · [Chronology](chronology.md) · [Ciphertext pages](../../pages/README.md) · [Sources](../../sources/README.md)

The intercepted messages record German forces leaving territories in which they could no longer assume control of transport, money or communications. Orders for withdrawal coexist with purchasing instructions, demands for pay, ship-sale negotiations and enquiries about people still on the road. References and repeated receptions trace decisions across successive transmissions; their wording distinguishes proposed arrangements from reported actions.

## Relations established across messages

A message number can join two instructions more securely than their position in the intercept book. The [shipping study](black-sea-shipping.md) follows the reference from the five-part **FR 52751** order into its later amendment, **FR 52787**. This establishes a revision of Siegfried's destination while leaving the anomalous header times visible.

A second reception can change the historical scale of a report. In the [Arad correspondence](../../pages/168.md#source-p168-e), it supplies the missing coordinates behind **80,000 men and 30,000 animals**. The [withdrawal study](withdrawal-transit-internment.md) places that stated strength beside the announced allocation of trains and the armistice transit provisions.

A completed instruction can also clarify the kind of problem being addressed. The [banknote witnesses](../../pages/195.md#edition-cases-finance-crown-banknote-completion) recover the request for small denominations; the [financial study](cash-procurement-liquidation.md) connects it with the distinction between a remittance and the money available for local payment. The [vehicle study](transport-demobilisation.md) similarly separates an order to be ready from a later report that a truck had left.

## External comparisons

The studies compare telegrams with armistice texts, operational maps, contemporary newspapers, a naval officer's diary, shipping registers and council records. Each comparison addresses a specific question: a timetable, a location, a movement, an ownership entry or a parallel version of a letter. The [source register](../../sources/README.md#historical-and-technical-context) identifies the material and its inspected scope; exact images and page locators accompany the individual arguments.
