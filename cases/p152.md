# p152 — Silk purchases and transport from the Caucasus

[Case index](README.md) · [ADFGVX Cipher](../README.md)

**Status:** source-linked body recovery. **Key:** Nov4–6. **Contribution:** icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro, 2 November 2026, identified the truncated challenge with the fuller p136 B transmission.

## Most likely reading

> Fuer kaiserliche deutsche Delegation, fuer MAIER HALLER, Tiflis. Nummer sechzehn. [Veranlassen, dass] im [Kaukasus] gekaufte Seide, Seidengarne, [Abfaelle], Strusen, [deren] Abtransport nicht unbedingt sichergestellt ist, nach Moeglichkeit dort wieder verkauft werden. Mercator. Kriegsamt, Kriegsrohstoffabteilung. R. 138 – elf. KRA.

**Meaning:** To the Imperial German delegation in Tiflis, for the person or designation written `MAIER HALLER`: arrange for silk, silk yarn, waste and coarse cocoon filaments bought in the Caucasus to be resold locally where their transport cannot be guaranteed. The message is signed Mercator and the War Office's raw materials department.

**Notes:** This readable reconstruction combines the fuller p136 first part with the continuation witnesses. Bracketed words normalize their garbles; `MAIER HALLER` is not silently changed to a rank or identified person. `Strusen` is an attested silk-trade term. The 104-slot p152 fragment alone does not yield this complete text; its relationship to p136 supplies the missing body.

The message concerns the Imperial German delegation in Tiflis and silk, silk yarn, waste, and *Strusen* purchased in the Caucasus. ## Evidence

At group alignment 210, the fragment and the full record share 88 known symbols, differ at 14, and have 2 erased positions. Restoring the 210-symbol prefix comes from the historical parallel. An exhaustive suffix edit comparison instead has cost 11 and four tied offsets, 211–214, each with 92 known-symbol matches. Neither model establishes a unique historic error sequence.
