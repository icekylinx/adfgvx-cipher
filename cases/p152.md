## Raw p136 B1 parallel plaintext

```text
FUERKAISERLICHEDEUTSCHEDELEGATIONFUERMAIERHALLERXTIFLISXNUMMERSECHSZEHNVERANLASSELXDGSSIMKAPKKSUSGEKAUFTEXSEIDEXSEIDENGARNEXABFAEHLEXSTRUSENXDETENABTRANSPORT
```

The message concerns the Imperial German delegation in Tiflis and silk, silk yarn, waste, and *Strusen* purchased in the Caucasus. Its continuation directs local resale where transport cannot be assured, and names Mercator and the War Office raw materials department. `MAIER HALLER` remains an unidentified literal reading. Garbles such as `KAPKKSUS` are preserved.
