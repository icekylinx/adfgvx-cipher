# p218 A - Kress, Tiflis and the armistice withdrawal condition

[ADFGVX Cipher](../../README.md) · [All readings](../README.md) · [Source records](../../experiments/corpus_readings/selected_sources.json) · [Reading conventions](../../docs/reading-conventions.md)

**Records:** p218 A1-A3, three parts of one message dated 25 November 1918.
**Result:** the treaty reference supports a conditional interpretation of
two Nov25–28 numeral cells. It reduces the reading's source cost from
**16 to 13 observed-coordinate substitutions**, with the same three erasure
fills. The two final digits of the opening telegram reference remain unknown.
The message concerns German troops in former Russian territory and the
German guard at Tiflis.

## Most likely reading

**Readable German — conditional treaty numeral interpretation:**

> General von Kress, Tiflis. Auf Tel. Nr. 2??. Artikel roem. 12
> Waffenstillstandsvertrages bestimmt: Alle deutschen Truppen, die sich
> gegenwaertig in Gebieten befinden, die vor dem Krieg zu Russland
> gehoerten, haben, sobald die Alliierten unter Beruecksichtigung der
> inneren Lage dieser Gebiete den Augenblick fuer gekommen erachten,
> in die deutschen Grenzen zurueckzukehren.
>
> Die Alliierten haben bisher die Zurueckziehung der deutschen
> Schutzwache aus Tiflis nicht verlangt. Auswaertiges Amt.

The article is XII. The transmitted instruction `ROEM12` expresses that
Roman-number reference; spacing and punctuation are editorial.

**Meaning:** Replying to telegram 163, the Foreign Office informs General
von Kress in Tiflis that the armistice requires German troops in territory
that belonged to Russia before the war to return within Germany's borders
when the Allies judge the moment suitable in light of local conditions.
The telegram adds that the Allies have not yet demanded withdrawal of the
German guard from Tiflis.

The wording identifies **Article XII** in the independently published
[German armistice text](https://germanhistorydocs.org/de/die-weimarer-republik-1918-1933/waffenstillstandsbedingungen-11-november-1918)
and the [U.S. diplomatic documentary edition](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1919Parisv02/d1).
The two received numeral pairs in `RO?MJ1` are complete: `FX` and `VG`.
Reading them as 1 and 2 gives `ROEM12`, after the existing missing `E`
fill. This [conditional shared-square interpretation](../../experiments/nov25_treaty_digits/README.md)
preserves three coordinates that the earlier numeral repair changed.
It also changes the opening raw-label reference `163` to `2??`; its
remaining two values are not established.

The same `FX=1` assignment gives the headquarters designation `AOK11`
in the [Breslau staff order](../people/breslau_staff_transfer.md) and both
11th-Army addresses in the [crown requests](../finance/crown_cash_requests.md).
These are consistent consequences of the treaty identification. The
published square, source transcriptions and earlier edition remain intact.

The telegram paraphrases the treaty. The final statement about the Tiflis
guard belongs to this telegram, not to Article XII. No conclusion about a
later withdrawal date is inferred from it.

## Source, route and credit

Printed p218 / PDF p221, heading **25 November 1918**:
`NKJ v LP (02.10 p.m., November 25th); FUER COS 0950 (11/25) 3 TLE`.
The three parts are separately labelled RICHI-282, RICHI-284 and RICHI-278.
Using the established recipient-first header convention, the transmission
is LP to NKJ, for onward delivery to COS. This is an additional Kress-route
record; it does not determine the key or disputed date of [p73](../p73.md).

The source is J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher
System*, in the [public scan](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view).
The published period key keeps its external attribution in
[data/keys.json](../../data/keys.json). Source inspection, assembly,
coordinate audit and this annotated edition are by
**icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro**. No first historical decipherment priority
is claimed.
