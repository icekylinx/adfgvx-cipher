## Most likely reading

**Readable German — earlier credited reconstruction under the recorded repairs:**

> [Fuer] Eilvese. Wiederholet Telegr. von vierter Periode in [fuenfter]. Gebet [vorher] 5 Min. VVV.

**Meaning:** For Eilvese: repeat the telegram from the fourth period in the fifth. Send `VVV` for five minutes beforehand.

**Notes:** [Norbert, 2017-03-12, comment 40](https://scienceblogs.de/klausis-krypto-kolumne/2017/02/23/the-top-50-unsolved-encrypted-messages-46-unsolved-adfgvx-cryptograms-from-world-war-1/?all=1#comment-858211) already proposed `GEBET VORHER` and observed that it needs one coordinate change, fewer than `GEBETSORDER`. The readable version also normalizes `FFER` → `FUER` and `FUTNFTER` → `FUENFTER`; together these change three plaintext letters without changing the length. The wording is an earlier proposal. **icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro** contribute its present source review and explicit coordinate-certificate presentation. `VVV` is retained as transmitted text, not expanded into a word.

## Review

- The 155-coordinate received text is odd. Norbert's model removes one V after inverse transposition, before pairing; it is not a received-ciphertext deletion.
- The marked extra V is intermediate 56. Adjacent identical Vs make the exact position within that run unidentifiable; removing intermediate 57 yields the same repaired stream.
- The displayed 2017 rectangle also changes source 88 A to X, beyond its two explicitly suggested erasure fillings. Without this extra change the raw word is FU1NFTER, not FUTNFTER.
