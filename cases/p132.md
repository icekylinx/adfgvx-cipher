# p132 — Repeat-transmission instructions for Eilvese

[Case index](README.md) · [Reading conventions](../docs/reading-conventions.md)

Childs p132 B, received on 5 November 1918, instructs Eilvese to repeat a telegram and precede it with a signal. The conditional reading uses the published **Nov4–6** key and a model in which one extra `V` entered the intermediate coordinate stream.

## Most likely reading

**Readable German — conditional extra-coordinate model, with three substitutions and two erasure fills:**

> Für Eilvese. Wiederholet Telegr[amm] von vierter Periode in fünfter.
> Gebet vorher 5 Min. VVV.

**Meaning:** For Eilvese: repeat the telegram from the fourth period during
the fifth. Send `VVV` for five minutes beforehand. The older imperatives
`WIEDERHOLET` and `GEBET` and the numerical `5` remain literal. `[amm]` only
expands the telegram abbreviation.

The [p134 repeat instruction](context/radio_instructions.md#p134-b--repeat-a-fourth-period-telegram-in-the-fifth) independently uses the fourth/fifth-period formula. The preceding `VORHER` signal clause is a p132 reconstruction, first proposed by Norbert in 2017.

### Exact costs and the four equivalent positions

| Received source index, zero-based | Printed group / position, one-based | Change | Effect |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 52 | 11 / 3 | `X` → `F` | `SORHER` → `VORHER` |
| 64 | 13 / 5 | `A` → `D` | `FFER` → `FUER` |
| 88 | 18 / 4 | `A` → `F` | `FU1NFTER` → `FUENFTER` |
| 103 | 21 / 4 | printed dash → `F` | Missing `E` in `TELEGR` |
| 104 | 21 / 5 | printed dash → `X` | Missing `N` in `VON` |

The repair of `FU1NFTER` changes the affected coordinate within the word; the square's digit `1` and the numeral in `5MIN` retain their assignments.

Re-encryption of the 77-character proposal reintroduces the extra intermediate `V` before applying the original column order. All four positions produce the same 155-slot witness with **three observed substitutions, two dash fills and one extra intermediate coordinate**. Their differing source origins remain in the certificate.

The [geometry audit](../experiments/november_radio_service/geometry_audit.json) compares all 156 one-insertion and 155 one-removal positions. For the stated wording, the four equivalent `V` removals form the unique minimum-cost set. Earlier insertion candidates and the full comparison remain in that audit; the result is conditional on the plaintext template.

## Review

Norbert's marked extra `V` is intermediate 56; adjacent identical coordinates make its position within the run indistinguishable. His displayed rectangle also changes source 88 from A to X. That additional change produces `FUTNFTER`; the unaltered source reads `FU1NFTER`. The current wording instead uses the A→F repair in the table above.
