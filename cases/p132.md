# p132 — Repeat-transmission instructions for Eilvese

[Case index](README.md) · [Reading conventions](../docs/reading-conventions.md)

**State: CONDITIONAL.** Childs p132 B; received 5 November 1918.
**Key:** `Nov4-6`. The reading uses one extra `V` in the intermediate
coordinate stream, three observed-coordinate substitutions and two printed
erasure fills. Four equivalent extra-`V` positions remain indistinguishable.

Thomas published the initial fragment in 2017; Norbert supplied the fuller
sender-error model and the `VORHER` alternative. Max Baertl published a
related editorial completion. **icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro** provide the
current source comparison, exact repair accounting and geometry audit.

## Most likely reading

**Readable German — conditional extra-coordinate model, with three substitutions and two erasure fills:**

> Für Eilvese. Wiederholet Telegr[amm] von vierter Periode in fünfter.
> Gebet vorher 5 Min. VVV.

**Meaning:** For Eilvese: repeat the telegram from the fourth period during
the fifth. Send `VVV` for five minutes beforehand. The older imperatives
`WIEDERHOLET` and `GEBET` and the numerical `5` remain literal. `[amm]` only
expands the telegram abbreviation.

This is a radio-service instruction about retransmission and a preceding
signal. The independent [p134 repeat instruction](context/radio_instructions.md#p134-b--repeat-a-fourth-period-telegram-in-the-fifth)
contains the fourth/fifth-period wording; it does not independently establish
p132’s proposed `VORHER` clause.

This page is the sole catalogue entry for the physical source. The older
identifier `p132_B` remains inside the linked source package as a verified
evidence alias. Its complete 155-slot transcription is identical to
challenge `p132`; [the identity register](../docs/catalogue-source-identity.md)
keeps that relationship explicit without adding another source.

### Exact costs and the four equivalent positions

| Received source index, zero-based | Printed group / position, one-based | Change | Effect |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 52 | 11 / 3 | `X` → `F` | `SORHER` → `VORHER` |
| 64 | 13 / 5 | `A` → `D` | `FFER` → `FUER` |
| 88 | 18 / 4 | `A` → `F` | `FU1NFTER` → `FUENFTER` |
| 103 | 21 / 4 | printed dash → `F` | Missing `E` in `TELEGR` |
| 104 | 21 / 5 | printed dash → `X` | Missing `N` in `VON` |

The raw numeral label `1` in `FU1NFTER` is retained in both raw readings.
Its change to `E` is an explicit local coordinate repair within a word; it
does not reassign the square's digit `1`. The numerical `5` in `5MIN` remains
unchanged.

The extra coordinate is separately charged as **one extraneous intermediate
coordinate**. It is not silently discarded from the received source. To
reproduce the 155-slot witness, encode the 77-character proposal, reinsert
the same `V` at the selected interim position, and apply the original rank
order. All four choices produce the same transmitted witness after the
five listed repairs/fills. Their differing source origins are retained in
the certificate; there is no uniquely identified extra `V`.

An initial insertion-only search retrieved the useful ending but left
`TELEGAR` or `TELERGR`. Those earlier candidates remain in the
[geometry audit](../experiments/november_radio_service/geometry_audit.json).
The later extra-coordinate interpretation was selected after inspecting
the source and wording. All 156 one-insertion and 155 one-removal interim
positions are preserved. For this retrospective 77-character template,
the four identical-V removals uniquely form the minimum-cost set of three
substitutions and two dash fills. This is a conditional consistency result,
not independent proof of the historical mechanism.

## Review

- The 155-coordinate received text is odd. Norbert's model removes one V after inverse transposition, before pairing; it is not a received-ciphertext deletion.
- The marked extra V is intermediate 56. Adjacent identical Vs make the exact position within that run unidentifiable; removing intermediate 57 yields the same repaired stream.
- The displayed 2017 rectangle also changes source 88 A to X, beyond its two explicitly suggested erasure fillings. Without this extra change the raw word is FU1NFTER, not FUTNFTER.
