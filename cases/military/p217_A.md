> Entwaffnung gestatten. Massow Budapest ist ersucht, letztere Fragen
> zu klaeren. OKM [damaged reference].

**Meaning:** The report locates troops withdrawing across Romania and
Hungary. Coal shortages have stopped outward transport. Some battalions
appear to have been misdirected; the Czechs refuse their stay and intend
to allow transit only after disarmament. Massow in Budapest has been asked
to clarify those questions.

The displayed numbers assume baseline `Y→1`, `J→2`, and `2→6`. The
contemporary French telegram identified below supports the 11th Army,
16th Landwehr, 226th Infantry and 6th Reserve Division identifications.
The same assignments yield *am 22* and R.I.R. 21. Several fields remain
damaged: `21?6 D` has not been replaced with an expected division number,
`POEST?EN` has not been given a definitive place spelling, and the number
of other battalions is not asserted. The certificate retains raw `KEENNE`;
the bracketed *keine* above is an editorial shortening outside the
same-length coordinate edition.

## Source, interpretation and credit

Printed p217 / PDF p220, heading **24 November 1918**:
`LP v NKJ (01.36 a.m., November 26th); VON UKS 2230 (11/24)
FUER LP (BSL) 5 TLE`. The original sender/recipient fields are retained.
The source is J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher
System*, in the [public scan](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view).

The historical number interpretation uses document 39, printed p79 / PDF
p153, in *Documents diplomatiques français sur l'histoire du Bassin des
Carpates, 1918–1932*, vol. I (Budapest, 1993): Franchet d'Esperey's
19 November 1918 telegram reporting German formations as of 11 November.
The [provenance record](../../experiments/p170_continuation/military_context.json)
identifies its source and hash. It is contextual evidence, not a printed
key or proof of every later formation location.

The optional map clears the other potential numeral/J/Q/Y labels and
preserves one global bijection. It never assigns both baseline `Y` and
baseline `J` to 1. Baseline labels, conditional domains and all source
edits are separately recorded. Published period-key credit remains in
[data/keys.json](../../data/keys.json). This source inspection and edition
are by **icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro**. No first historical decipherment
