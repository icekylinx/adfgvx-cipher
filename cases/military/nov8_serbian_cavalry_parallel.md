## What the additional source establishes

Printed p158 E1 has **235 coordinates against an advertised 236**. Its
third source row contains the four-coordinate group `XDGV`. The independent
p170 B1 copy has `AXDGV` in the corresponding position. Adding its observed
`A` restores a 236-coordinate frame while retaining all 235 p158 coordinates
in their printed order. The source-linked frame fixes **117 of 118
plaintext positions** and preserves three letter disagreements with p170.

The report describes Serbian cavalry entering the places transmitted as
`PETERWADEIN` and `FEHERTEMPLOM`, followed by a northward departure of
`217 I D` and `? R D`. The spelling `PETERWADEIN` lacks the R in the fuller
place-name used by another report. The final `FEINBERUEHRUNG` appears to
mean enemy contact but lacks the D of `FEIND`; the exact readable stream
retains both omissions. `ANENMELDUNG` remains unexpanded.

The conservative edited layer uses **five observed-coordinate
substitutions**: three are supported by the independent raw copy; two are
shared ordinary-word repairs. The added A is separately priced as **one
source-linked coordinate addition**, not represented as a coordinate
visible on p158. No source coordinate is excluded, reordered or masked.
