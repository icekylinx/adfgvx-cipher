## What the geometry search establishes

The search uses eight retrospective word spans at the offsets listed below. It does not constrain the references, other surrounding words or the entire proposed body. It prices observed-coordinate conflicts, recording mask fills and source exclusions separately.

All **375 contiguous ten-slot exclusions** were tested. The minimum is **seven observed substitutions**, at starts **4–13** inclusive. These are all retained, not just the six source maps yielding the primary ciphertext.

A second, narrower source-group model tests all **2,850 pairs of complete five-coordinate groups** among the first 76 groups. The final four-coordinate group is retained. Exactly two pairs attain the same seven-substitution minimum: zero-based groups **[1,2]** and **[2,3]**, corresponding to source intervals **[5,15)** and **[10,20)**. They remove the intervening -XVVG and one of the repeated VGXXV groups and produce the same shortened ciphertext.

These two whole-group descriptions support selecting a reproducible primary display. They do not prove a unique historical deletion, nor exclude other damage models. All words and offsets were chosen retrospectively; this is not a blind decipherment test.

**Conditional interpretation — aviation movement arrangements:**

The proposed body gives Buckarest as the march destination for the staff marked KOFL MACK and Ploesti for a numerically restricted army aviation park, followed by a railway reference to Kronstadt and Bucarest. It also refers to an earlier communication to KOGENLUFT. This reading does not identify a specific historical order, establish its execution, or expand the aviation abbreviations into independently verified offices.

GEDRATET is retained without inserting an H, BUCKAREST is retained beside the later BUCAREST, and MARSCHZ is not expanded. J43, X4OP, J, X and XX remain literal numeral/letter labels. No date, reference number or personal identity is inferred from them. The conditional reading’s ordinary words do not resolve the competing layouts outside the stated eight spans.
