Childs p141 B, PDF p144, directs any personnel being returned from
Seeflugstation Ost, formerly Sewastopol, to the second Seefliegerabteilung.
It explicitly says they should not be sent to the first. Its heading is
`ASO v LP (08.19 p.m., November 6th)`, followed by
`FUER ASO 1920 (6/11)`.

editing, although three resulting symbols require ordinary-word repairs.

## Conditional interpretation and limits

The instruction concerns personnel **if returned**; it does not establish
that a transfer actually took place. `S.F.A.` is retained as the telegram's
abbreviation, and **GX4774XROEMX1** remains the literal reference. No new
formation number, administrative interpretation or numerical relabeling is
needed to read the contrast between the second and first detachments.

The complete candidate re-encrypts to the source after exactly those
three substitutions. There is no insertion, deletion, erasure fill,
frame change or key change. The prior repository screen already contained
this body with OCR-induced uncertainties; this edition resolves the
source transcription and makes the editorial changes independently checkable.
