## A: request for armistice conditions

The conditional proposal asks OHL for the special armistice conditions applying to this theatre. `BESX` remains an abbreviation. The raw body has `AVK1P`; the edition changes the ordinary letter V to O but preserves the suffix `1P` as `AOK1P`. Its footer separately reads `A X O X K X 11`. The thematic request on p179 C is not used to normalize these unit labels.

The second source row ends `DDFXXK`: the final slot is a crossed/overtyped K- or X-like mark, not a certain extra coordinate. It is retained in the literal transcription at zero-based source index 80 and excluded from the 224-coordinate working frame. If it is a noncoordinate overprint, no known coordinate is deleted; if read as X, the exclusion costs one observed-coordinate deletion. Neither this geometry nor the wording has been shown globally optimal or unique. Under the X interpretation, neighboring identical X values can also make the identity of an excluded mark ambiguous.

The third source row ends `GDGAG AV?XF`. The [enlarged source crop](../../experiments/armistice_provisions_requests/sources/p188_A_damaged_glyph.png) preserves the vertical/overlapping strokes in the third glyph of the last group, source index 118. The complete row and the original embedded image pixels do not establish an unqualified X. The earlier frozen OCR also recorded `AV?XF`; that is another transcription of the same witness, not an independent copy. No literal M is asserted. The source mark therefore remains `?`, alongside the five printed dashes and the B-shaped glyph at source index 165: seven source masks in total.

Source index 118 maps to working index 117 and pairs with source index 90 at plaintext position 42. Its raw `?V` pair has domain `234789ACMQVXY`, so the former raw C in `BESCNZ` is now `?` in `BES?NZ`. The edition still proposes D at that source slot, producing `DV = X` in `BESX`; this is a mask fill. The certificate separately retains the **conditional X interpretation**: `XV = C`, 105 raw fixed positions, 15 observed substitutions and six fills, with the identical edited stream. Reclassifying this damaged glyph reduces the number of observed substitutions by one and increases fills by one; it does not recover a letter or reduce the total number of proposed coordinate changes.

## B2: provisions available for only five days

The edited text says that the need is very urgent and provisions last at most five more days. `LAENGSTENSOCH` and `FELDINTENTENDANT` are retained exactly in the edited stream: no missing-looking N is inserted and no duplicated-looking syllable is deleted. The V before AOK11 and the reference `6011` remain baseline labels. This is a statement in the telegram, not independent verification of the supply situation.

All 176 received slots are present, yet the source explicitly says “jammed to end.” That interference note remains part of the record; no unprinted tail is invented. Four raw question marks here arise from unassigned square cells, not source erasures; their proposed letters require the counted coordinate changes.

## C1: flour and oats to Szolnok

The proposal asks the Generalintendant of the field army to send a train at an unknown interval, with an unresolved number of full portions including flour and the remainder oats, to Szolnok on the Theiss. Four observed-coordinate substitutions produce `EINSCHL`, `SZOLNOK` and `ANDER`; the quantity and dispatch interval are not inferred. The raw `VVZUG` abbreviation stays unchanged.

The clearly printed E in `EFXGF` is outside the coordinate alphabet and remains unfilled at source index 140. All four unresolved plaintext positions (zero-based) remain explicit:
