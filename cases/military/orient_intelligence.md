# Orient intelligence: expected landings and fighting on the Tigris

The report corrects an earlier Orient note, assesses shipping and expected landings around Alexandretta, and relays reports of an attack on Turkish positions near Kalat Shergat. Its observations and second-hand allegations remain distinguished in the edition.

The unchanged Nov1–3 square and ranks are previously published.
Parts one, five and six also predate this edition as readable unreviewed
repository candidates. The incorrectly merged p93 candidate was
unreadable; its exact prior output is frozen in the inputs. The new
contribution is the source transcription, separated part frames,
complete report assembly and reversible repairs.

The Lasry et al. paper and dissertation discuss Foreign Armies
intelligence generally. The bounded check did not locate an explicit
discussion of this six-part body, and the cached comment-thread check
found no such body. That does not establish decipherment priority.

and **1,010 plaintext positions**. There are **1,009 fixed raw positions**;
the explicit candidates fix all 1,010 at a total cost of **four observed
substitutions and one printed-erasure fill**. No key, rank or source
length is changed. All dates, unit numbers, convoy figures and distance
