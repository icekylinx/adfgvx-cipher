## p217 B1

**Readable German — zero-substitution lexical completion under the conditional frame; literal labels retained:**

> ABENDMEL7G. ALP KORPS 8 weitere Zuege abgerollt. JY0 ID unv?raendert. 2? RD Anfang Oroshaza. ?Y? Anfang B?KR0?SABA Z.

The intact central proposal says that further trains have departed. The later
passage places an `RD` leading element at Oroshaza. The labels `8`, `JY0`,
`2?`, `?Y?` and final `Z` are retained; no historical train count or unit
number is assigned. The question marks are unresolved positions.

The source prints 204 slots, including 21 masks. It genuinely repeats `FXXXF`
at source indices 45–49 and 50–54. The conditional frame excludes the first
copy and inserts five unknown slots before retained-stream index 12. No
cancellation mark is printed, and neither operation is an established
historical correction.
