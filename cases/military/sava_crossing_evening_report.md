> Fuer O.H.L. Abendmeldg. Save ist von 30 I.D. mit Hauptteilen, von G.K. 39 ganz ueberschritten.

This is an evening report to `O.H.L.`. It says that `30 I.D.` has crossed
the Sava with its main elements and that `G.K. 39` has crossed completely.
The comma reflects the compressed telegram syntax. The text retains the
literal abbreviated unit designations and both numbers; it does not identify
particular formations from outside history. `ABENDMELDG` is not silently
expanded inside the quoted display.

## Possible relation to the p100 challenge

The [p100 A header crop](../../experiments/nov1_contact_mail_and_sava/sources/p100_A_header.png)
has the same `UKS v ZON` direction and `2016 (1)` origin. Its body is the
existing [p100 challenge](../p100.md), whose earlier reading describes no
enemy interference and two enemy divisions marching on Belgrade.

The source headings are not identical: **p99 says `3 TLE`, while p100 says
`2 TLE`**. They support a possible multipart relation, but this edition does
not silently reinterpret the latter as an unambiguous part number, identify
an absent third part, or merge the catalog entries. The earlier p100 body
remains credited to **Armin, 2017-03-01, comment 13**. The current p99 source
edition does not claim a new solution of that challenge.
