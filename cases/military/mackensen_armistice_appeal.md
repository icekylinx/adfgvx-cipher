# Mackensen's appeal over disarmament and internment

[Catalogue](../README.md) · [Reading conventions](../../docs/reading-conventions.md)

**Prior body identification:** Childs; summarized by George Lasry, Ingo
Niebel, Nils Kopal and Arno Wacker (2016). **Source edition and exact repair
audit:** icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro.

This six-part message reports Berthelot's communication of Foch's refusal
to apply the Western armistice conditions to the German southeastern front,
and a demand for disarmament and internment of Mackensen's army group.
The proposed German edition distinguishes acknowledging receipt while a
decision is pending, disarmament by Hungary, and a request to forgo
internment. Its fifth part is a conditional reconstruction of a genuinely
short source frame. The closing warns of mass flight, misery and open
resistance.

The [conditional Article XII numeral interpretation](../../experiments/nov25_treaty_digits/README.md)
clarifies four positions in the closing reference: raw `JAJJ6J4` becomes
`1A11?1?`. It adds no source change. The earlier repairs of raw
`MITGETEILJ` and `SOLLJE` remain necessary; those received `J` labels would
otherwise become 1 under the same assignment.

The source is [Childs's collection](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view),
printed **p220 C1–C4 and p221 A5–A6**, PDF pages 223–224. The repeated header
links the six parts:

none is inserted.

Read with C4, the stated candidate asks that internment be **forgone**,
while its intervening clause says that provisioning would make Hungary
the only feasible location. The wording is more specific than a summary
that merely proposes internment in Hungary. This interpretation inherits
the explicit missing-prefix and ordinary-word assumptions; the absent
coordinates are not independently witnessed by another transmission.

### All declared loss placements are retained

The audit checks all **223** placements of one ten-coordinate gap and all
**24,976** ordered placements of two five-coordinate gaps. The complete
retrospective candidate has a unique minimum of six observed-coordinate
changes when both missing groups precede source index 0.

That is not independent proof of the location. Masking `ABSTANDGENOMMENWIRD`,
`INTERNIERUNG` and `KARPATHEN` leaves five two-group minima, each with four
changes on the remaining template: **(0,0), (1,1), (1,2), (2,2), (3,3)**.
All are preserved. Their raw readings share **100 of 116 fixed positions**.
The book and radio scores also rank the leading gap first, but language
ranking is used only to find a candidate. Other loss mechanisms and an
incorrect length indicator are not excluded by these bounded comparisons.
