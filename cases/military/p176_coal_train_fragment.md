## Why propose these words?

The printed header and consecutive part labels put A5 immediately before A6
in the same six-part message. Its previously edited ending begins
`KANNMITZUFUEHRUNGVON` and ends with the literal conjunction `UND`.
That syntax motivates dative plural `KOHLENZUEGEN` at the start of A6.
The exact native half-coordinate constraints also permit
`UEBERKLAUSENBURG` at positions 12–27 and `GERECHNETWERDEN` at 41–55.
These words are contextual proposals; compatibility does not prove them.
