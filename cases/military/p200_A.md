# p200 A — Siedlce garrisons reach Thorn

**Status: complete source-level reading with explicit repairs.** The
165-character message reports that the Siedlce and Minsk Maziowicki
garrisons have reached Thorn, while the whereabouts of Battalion Ostrowo
are unknown. It is signed General von Zedlitz, hitherto military governor
of Siedlce.

The Nov13–15b square and column order are prior published work by
**George Lasry, Ingo Niebel, Nils Kopal and Arno Wacker**. This source
edition, repair certificate and independent identity check are by
**icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro**. No first historical decipherment
priority is claimed.

## Reading

The unchanged source and key give:

```text
ANGENERALG0UVERNEMENTWARSCHAUXGARISONXSIEDLCEXUNDMINSKMAZIOWICKIINXTHORJXEINGETROFFENVERBLEIBBWTLXOSTRVKOXNECHTBEKANNTXGENERALVZEDLIIZBI?HERMILIEAERGOUV?RNEURVIEDLCE
```

The proposed, same-length edition is:

```text
ANGENERALGOUVERNEMENTWARSCHAUXGARISONXSIEDLCEXUNDMINSKMAZIOWICKIINXTHORNXEINGETROFFENVERBLEIBBATLXOSTROWOXNICHTBEKANNTXGENERALVZEDLITZBISHERMILITAERGOUVERNEURSIEDLCE
```

In readable English: “To General Government Warsaw. The garrisons of
Siedlce and Minsk Maziowicki have arrived in Thorn. The whereabouts of
Battalion Ostrowo are unknown. General von Zedlitz, hitherto military
governor of Siedlce.” Punctuation and expansion of `BATL` are editorial.
The literal spellings `GARISON` and `MINSKMAZIOWICKI` are retained in the
cryptographic edition, without adding normalization letters. No battalion
number or signer's forename is inferred.

include source errors and are not a count of correct German letters.

## Independent identity evidence

A contemporary bilingual notice in **Kreisblatt für den Kreis Grojec /
Dziennik rozporządzeń dla powiatu Grójeckiego**, no. 29, **15 October
1918**, printed p211 / PDF p4, is dated **Siedlce, 16 September 1918**
and signed by military governor **Freiherr von Zedlitz, Generalmajor**.
The [signature](../../experiments/siedlce_withdrawal/sources/grojec_1918_29_p211_signature.png)
and [issue title](../../experiments/siedlce_withdrawal/sources/grojec_1918_29_title.png)
were visually verified in the [public scan](http://mbc.cyfrowemazowsze.pl/Content/15317/15317.pdf).
This supports the proposed signer's name and office. It does not
independently attest the troop movements reported in the telegram.
