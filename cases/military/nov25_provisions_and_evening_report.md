# Reduced provisioning strength and a separate evening report

**Reading layers:** [Raw output · Edited output · Readable German · Conditional interpretation](../../docs/reading-conventions.md). In decoded streams, `?` marks one unresolved position; `X` and numeral/letter labels remain literal.

**Source review and edition: icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro.**

Two physically numbered parts request a complete stop to provisions after a reduction in the strength being provisioned. Their association is explicit across the p219/p220 page break. A separate evening report on p220 preserves its unresolved unit and place labels. All three use their complete native printed frames and the unchanged Nov25–28 key.

## p219 D1

**Source transcription — header:** `D) UKS v ZÖN (10.11 p.m., November 25th); 1849 2 TLE; 1 TL RICHI-166`

**Raw output — every native source slot in printed order, with non-coordinate glyphs masked:**

```text
INFOLGEVERRINGERTERXVERPFLEGUNGSSTAERKEFUERJJXARMEEKEINEVERPFLEGNNGFEHRERFOGDERLICH
```

**Edited output — 3 observed-coordinate substitutions and 0 source-mask fills; no insertion, deletion or reordering:**

```text
INFOLGEVERRINGERTERXVERPFLEGUNGSSTAERKEFUERJJXARMEEKEINEVERPFLEGUNGMEHRERFORDERLICH
```

**Readable German — exact nonseparator labels of the edited output:**

> Infolge verringerter Verpflegungsstaerke fuer JJ. Armee keine Verpflegung mehr erforderlich.

**Meaning:** The message states that reduced provisioning strength makes further provisions unnecessary for the army designated by the literal JJ label. It does not identify that army by converting J to a decimal digit.

**Editorial limits.**

- All 166 observed coordinates are present, including the final printed VGGXA F omitted by the earlier 160-coordinate OCR block. Restoring those visible coordinates is a transcription correction, not a hypothetical lost-prefix or missing-slot fill.
- The word proposals VERPFLEGNNG → VERPFLEGUNG, FEHR → MEHR and ERFOGDERLICH → ERFORDERLICH have explicit observed-coordinate costs. All raw garbles remain displayed.
- JJ is retained literally. The edition supplies no army identity, headcount, or numerical key reassignment.
- The printed continued marker, identical UKS/ZÖN routing, 10.11 p.m. interception, 1849 internal reference and 2 TLE count associate this first part with p220 A2. The source changes its section label from D to A across the page break.

```text
BITTENACHSCHUBANVERPFLEGUNGSOFORTGANZEINSTELLENXGLEICHESTELEGRAMMGEHTANGENERALINTENDANTUNDPROVIANTDEPOTDRESDENXXVIERAXU203
```

**Readable German — exact nonseparator labels of the edited output:**

> Bitte Nachschub an Verpflegung sofort ganz einstellen. Gleiches Telegramm geht an Generalintendant und Proviantdepot Dresden. VIERA. U203.

**Meaning:** The continuation requests an immediate, complete halt to further provisions and states that the same telegram goes to the Generalintendant and the provisions depot at Dresden. It records a request and notification, without proving that deliveries actually stopped. The closing labels VIERA and U203 remain unexpanded.

**Editorial limits.**

- The complete native 244-coordinate body matches its header. Two earlier OCR blocks split this one physical source part; neither block alone gives its full geometry.
- SOFOR3 → SOFORT and GENENALINTENDANT → GENERALINTENDANT are separately priced ordinary-word proposals, with no global reinterpretation of the 3 label.
- VIERA and U203 remain literal closing fields. They are not silently converted to an office, a personal name, a number or an expanded date.
- The source explicitly marks this as part 2 and repeats the p219 D header. Its association with that first part is supported by printed metadata as well as the continued marker, not solely by similar meaning.
