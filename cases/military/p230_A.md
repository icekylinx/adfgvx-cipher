## Divisions and headquarters movement

**Conditional interpretation — readable German with the shared calendar and unit assignments:**

> 226. I. D. nordwestl. Hermannstadt. 16. Ldw. Div. um Muehlbach.
> 218. Div. im Raume Hatszeg, Livazeny. Drittens. Gen. Kdo. 63 Kronstadt.
> 89 und 3L3 Div. um Kronstadt. 7 bayr. Kav. Brig. um Fogaras versammelt.
> OKM verlegt Hauptquart. nach Muehlbach. OKM1ANRZ11U30OP.

**Meaning:** The report gives the positions of withdrawing divisions,
places General Command 63 at Kronstadt, and announces an OKM headquarters
move to Muehlbach. The shared interpretation now identifies the **7th Bavarian
cavalry brigade** around Fogaras. The damaged division field `3L3` and closing
reference remain literal.

The [joint date and unit study](../../experiments/late_november_calendar_and_units/README.md)
assigns `VG=7` from this brigade field and the `217` formation in p229 A1,
with support from Barkóczy-Klopsch's 1923 military history. The November date
in p229 separately fixes `GA=0`. These two assignments bring part 4 from
**113 to 115/115 interpreted positions**, without adding source edits.

The numbers above assume a consistent interpretation of baseline labels:
`X→1`, `J→2`, `8→6`, `4→8`, `5→9`, and `2→3`. Contemporary French intelligence
lists the 226th Infantry, 16th Landwehr, 218th Infantry and 89th Infantry
divisions. The French list itself marks the 89th as `89e D.I.?`; the later
independent Hungarian account lists the 89th without that query. The reading
of General Command 63 is supported by the p163
withdrawal edition under its stated historical and editorial assumptions.
These are conditional identifications, not cipher-only proofs.

The conventional separator `X` additionally assumes baseline `Y→X`.
The `Y` in *bayr.* and *Livazeny* assumes baseline `7→Y`; the latter place
also requires a counted `C→Z` repair. A fourth lexical layer keeps the
unchanged `Q` in the `QUART` abbreviation as `Q`. None of these lexical or
separator assumptions is silently included in the historical numeric map.

## Source and credit

Printed p230 / PDF p233, heading **30 November 1918**:
`BSL v ZON (09.26 p.m., December 1st); 2200; 1st and 2d TL missing`.
The missing parts have not been reconstructed. The source is J. Rives
Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, in the
[public scan](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view).

document 39, printed p79 / PDF p153 in *Documents diplomatiques français
sur l'histoire du Bassin des Carpates, 1918–1932*, vol. I (Budapest, 1993).
The [provenance record](../../experiments/p170_continuation/military_context.json)
identifies the edition and scan. This telegram reports formations as of
11 November; it does not directly establish their locations at month end.
