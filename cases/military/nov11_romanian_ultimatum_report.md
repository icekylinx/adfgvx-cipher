## p180 D1

**Readable German — conditional 196-slot reconstruction; six observed-source exclusions, five added-slot fills, ten printed-mask fills and two observed substitutions:**

> An OHL. Abendmeldung. Rumaen. Ultimatum forderte Raeumung 24 Stunden vor der Waffenstreckung. Nicht beantwortet.

An evening report to OHL concerns a Romanian ultimatum demanding evacuation
and says it was not answered. `24 Stunden vor der Waffenstreckung` is the
literal time phrase; the edition does not turn it into a calendar deadline.
`Rumaen.` displays the transmitted abbreviation `RUMAEN` without adding
letters. The related [p175 report](romanian_ultimatum_companions.md) concerns
an ultimatum too, but supplies no copied text or coordinates here.

## Source

Childs, printed p180 / PDF p183, section D1. The page has `(?) NOVEMBER, 1918`
with a handwritten `11th`. The header reads
`D) LP v ZON (03.49 p.m., November 11th); 22.20; 1 TL; RICHI-196`.
The originating-time field has no separately printed date. The source note
is `fairly correct - badly jammed`.
