# Parallel Orient reports on South-Slav units

**Reading layers:** [Raw output · Edited output · Readable German · Conditional interpretation](../../docs/reading-conventions.md). In decoded streams, `?` marks one unresolved position; `X` and numeral/letter labels remain literal.

Prepared by **icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro**.

Childs pp101–102 preserve closely related reports on South-Slav units near
Montenegro. The p102 report is an explicitly numbered three-part message.
The p101 material has a damaged first part and a matching detachment passage,
but some headers and wording differ. The edition preserves each physical
record instead of merging those differences.

The corresponding p102 B1 text below says **in** Montenegro. The source
coordinates support this wording difference. The p102 date `28` is not
transferred into the two unresolved p101 positions.

### Copy agreement and extra shared-word repair

The two detachment records have **192 identical observed coordinates**,
**seven observed conflicts**, and **one complementary mask**. All
**128 minimum common streams** are preserved in the certificate. Their
aggregate substitution cost is seven, and they all retain the shared raw
word error `SUEUSLAVISCHEN`.

The chosen complete candidate costs three substitutions in p101 B2 and
six in p102 B2, for **nine combined substitutions**, plus the one p101 source
mask fill. The two operations above the minimum are the additional shared
source-44 change `A`→`G`, once in each copy, to obtain `SUEDSLAVISCHEN`.
Thus the fluent word is an additional priced repair; the copies do not
independently confirm it through an unchanged coordinate. The body wording,
unit labels and all individual conflicting values remain inspectable.
