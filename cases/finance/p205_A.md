# A request for thirty million kroner

**Reading layers:** [Raw output · Edited output · Readable German · Conditional interpretation](../../docs/reading-conventions.md). In decoded streams, `?` marks one unresolved position; `X` and numeral/letter labels remain literal.

[ADFGVX Cipher](../../README.md) · [All readings](../README.md) · [Source and verification](../../experiments/corpus_joint/README.md)

**Record:** p205 A, four parts, intercepted on 17 November 1918 with an internal `1600 (16)` time/date. **Result:** the published Nov16–18 partial key fixes **481 of 486 plaintext positions** from all **972 printed coordinates**. The financial instructions are readable; five characters in the closing references remain unknown.

## Most likely reading

> Zur Geldanforderung bei OHL: Sendung von dreißig Millionen Kronen angesichts starken Notenmangels nur in Teilbeträgen möglich. Wiener Bankverein, Wien, überweist heute telegraphisch an Oesterr.-Ungarische Bank, Kronstadt, zur Verfügung deutscher Etappenverwaltung zunächst fünf Millionen gemischter Stückelung. Drahtet, ob glatte Auszahlung erfolgte und ob dieser Betrag vorläufig genügt. Sonst anfordert weitere Summe bei General-Kriegskasse, Zweigstelle Berlin. K … BD Nr. 1… Strich 11 B…

**English meaning:** Concerning the request to the Supreme Army Command for money: a shipment of thirty million kroner can only be made in installments because banknotes are in short supply. The Wiener Bankverein in Vienna is transferring an initial five million, in mixed denominations, to the Austro-Hungarian Bank at Kronstadt for the German rear-area administration. The recipient should telegraph whether payment went smoothly and whether the amount is sufficient for the time being; otherwise, further funds should be requested from the General War Treasury's Berlin branch.

Word spacing, punctuation and capitalization are editorial. Three obvious-looking word repairs still require changes to valid printed coordinates: `Z2R → ZUR`, `ETAPPENVWRWALTUNG → ETAPPENVERWALTUNG`, and `BERL2N → BERLIN`. They are kept separate from the raw decipherment. No value is supplied for any of the five unknown closing-reference characters.

## Source assembly and credit

The four printed parts are reproduced in the [p205 A crop](../../experiments/corpus_joint/sources/p205_A.png). The OCR split A1 after its first eight groups and appended a nonexistent extra unknown character to A4. Joining the source's A1 lines and removing that OCR artifact restores the printed geometries of 236 and 198 coordinates; neither operation deletes a printed coordinate. A2's source group 16 is visibly `FXAFF`, replacing an OCR question mark but retaining the printed `A` that causes the `VWRWALTUNG` garble.

The historical source is J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, printed p. 205, PDF p. 208 in the [complete scan](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view). Its header is `NKJ v LP`, with `FUER UKS` and `O K M HERMANNSTADT`; these routing details do not change the supplied plaintext.

The period key retains the attribution in [data/keys.json](../../data/keys.json) to **George Lasry** and the historical/research work of **George Lasry, Ingo Niebel, Nils Kopal and Arno Wacker**. Source assembly, review, exact coordinate audit and this edition are by **icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro**. No first historical decipherment priority is claimed.
