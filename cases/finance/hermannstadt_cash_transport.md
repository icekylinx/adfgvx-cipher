# Cash transport to Hermannstadt

Prepared by **icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro**.

Childs p216 B is a two-part message addressed to K M Berlin. Its origin is printed as 1715 on 24 November 1918; the receiving header is 01.30 a.m. on 26 November. The edition retains both dates and uses the published Nov22–24 key unchanged.

## p216 B1

If the aircraft at Döberitz has not started, the message asks for money to be sent promptly under special guard by train through Szolnok and Arad. The literal four-character group `AAAV` leaves one coordinate missing. Two placements before or after its last V have equal minimum repair cost and the same repaired ciphertext; both source mappings are retained.

Raw, with the gap placed after the short group:

```text
FALLSFLUGZEUGDOEBERITZN2IHTGESTART2TXGELDUMGE?ENDUNTERBEMONDERERBEWSCHUNGMITXVXZ?GUEBERXSZOLNOKXARADX
```

Conditional ordinary-word edition:

```text
FALLSFLUGZEUGDOEBERITZNICHTGESTARTETXGELDUMGEHENDUNTERBESONDERERBEWACHUNGMITXVXZUGUEBERXSZOLNOKXARADX
```

This costs five changes to observed coordinates, one fill of a printed dash, and one missing-coordinate fill. The full 202-position geometry screen is certified; the candidate itself is not an independent missing-character witness.

## p216 B2

The second part supplies the destination Hermannstadt, asks for arrival notifications, and retains the unexpanded phrase `KROGEHENHIERZUENDE`.

```text
HERMANNSTADTXSENDENXANKUNFTAUFDEN8STATIONENOKM9EWEILSMITTEILENXKROGEHENHIERZUENDEXOKMXYAYY?18XX
```

No source coordinate changes are made. Raw `8`, `9`, the reference labels and the final unresolved erasure remain visible. The raw 9 in `9EWEILS` is an independently observed `GD` pair at source positions 116 and 185. `JEWEILS` is a plausible separate lexical interpretation, not an alteration in this reading page.

The unreviewed corpus screen already recognized this second part. The present contribution is the source edition, the first part's conditional reconstruction, exact repair costs, and complete uncertainty record. No first historical decipherment claim is made.
