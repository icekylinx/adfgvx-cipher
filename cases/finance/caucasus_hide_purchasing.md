# Caucasus hide purchasing — Meierkort, the contract and the Kiew instruction

Hide purchasing is assigned exclusively to Meierkort, which is asked to return the signed contract promptly. Purchases are to continue while expenses are settled later; Wolters is instructed to report in Kiev.

**Source edition and review: icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro.** Three numbered
parts on pp. 75–76 instruct that hide purchasing is assigned exclusively to
`MEIERKORT`, ask for a signed contract to be returned, keep purchases running
while expenses are settled later, and direct the person in the `WOLTERS`
field to report in Kiew. The names are literal source readings; this edition
does not independently identify the firm or the person's full name.

All three parts repeat the Berlin KRA reference `1521 28/10`, address the
Caucasus representative of the War Ministry, and carry the same reception
time. The typed frames have 836 slots and fix 417 of 418 raw plaintext
positions. Their edited readings need six observed-coordinate substitutions
and one printed-dash fill. An additional 20-slot handwritten annotation on
p76 is preserved as a separate source layer below.

### Typed text and handwritten annotation

The [detail image](../../experiments/late_october_radio_and_materials/sources/p76_A3_annotation_detail.png)
contains **238 typed slots**, including one printed dash, and **20 additional
handwritten slots**. After the typed group `FDADA`, handwriting supplies
`FXXFF FDGAD DDDDF AVVFA`; the next four typed groups repeat that sequence.
The working reading uses the 238-slot typed layer, whose length independently
matches `RICHI-238`.

The central handwritten glyph in `DDDDF`, composite source index **87**, is
rounded enough to support **D or O**. `D` is the primary transcription, and
the following typed `DDDDF` supports the duplicate-annotation interpretation;
it does not independently settle that handwritten shape. The alternative
noncoordinate `O` is preserved as a masked diagnostic. Neither reading affects
the typed-only plaintext or its repair ledger.

This layer separation is explicit. If all 258 composite slots are instead
treated as transmitted material, obtaining the typed-only frame requires
excluding **20 slots**, containing **20 observed coordinates under D** or
**19 observed coordinates and one noncoordinate mask under O**. That is a
separate conditional model, not a zero-cost deletion of transmitted symbols.
The certificate retains both composite decodes and their full position maps.
