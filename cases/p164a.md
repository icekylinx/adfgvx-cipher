# p164a — Damaged Temesvar and MIRCO fragments

[Case index](README.md) · [ADFGVX Cipher](../README.md) · [Reading conventions](../docs/reading-conventions.md)

This damaged Nov7–9-key message contains a proposed reference to an occupation army at Temesvar and to routes west and south of `MIRCO`. Our new reading **BESATZUNGSHEER** agrees with all eighteen observed coordinate halves in positions 18–31. Its ten mask fills change no observed coordinate; the word also occurs in independently decoded p66 and p115 messages.

## Most likely partial reading

**Readable German — earlier conjectural fragments; display omissions are not counted positions:**

> […] [in Temesvar] […] [die von MIRCO nach Westen und Sueden … Wege … vom Gegner besetzt]. […]

**Meaning:** The earlier reconstruction refers to Temesvar and appears to report enemy occupation of routes west and south of `MIRCO`. The central clause and its grammar remain unresolved; a later suggestion reads `DIV VON MIRCO` instead of `DIE VON MIRCO`.

These bracketed clauses come from Norbert's 2017 proposal. Their grammar and the identity of `MIRCO` remain unresolved. The new occupation-army word replaces his earlier `BEFRIEDNISXE??` span; the complete revised stream is linked above.
