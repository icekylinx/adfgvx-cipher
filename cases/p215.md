## Most likely partial interpretation

> […] bringt [Entente-Flotte] zwo [Divisionen] […] [Neuseelaender], engl. u. [franz. Truppen?] […]

**Meaning:** The damaged passage most plausibly concerns an Entente fleet bringing two divisions, with mentions of New Zealand, English and French troops. The opening, the connection between these descriptions, and the closing command/signature remain uncertain.

**Notes:** A fluent complete plaintext is not supported. The quotation above is an interpretive synopsis of recognizable fragments, not an accepted repair. The exact raw reading has `ENT?N?EFLHTTE`, `ZWO?IVI?IONEN`, `?EUSRELAEND?R` and `F?ATZ?`. Baertl's fuller proposal requires at least 27 changes to known coordinates even after optimizing its extra-letter deletion and insertion geometry. The separately retained language-ranked completions are source-compatible hypotheses, not newly recovered letters.
