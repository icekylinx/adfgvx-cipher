# Siegfried's assignment to the Military Mission is cancelled

**Reading layers:** [Raw output · Edited output · Readable German · Conditional interpretation](../../docs/reading-conventions.md). In decoded streams, `?` marks one unresolved position; `X` and numeral/letter labels remain literal.

**Source edition and verification: icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro.**

The source's 401-coordinate frame yields a clear first portion followed by a
pairing-phase failure. A conditional surplus-X model recovers the rest of the
order, with an exact source-shaped forward witness. Its complete closing has
independent, unedited coordinate-pair support from the previously reviewed
Osthoff order. Neither the error model nor the shared formula assigns a
biographical identity to Siegfried or Marschall.

## A conditional surplus coordinate before transposition

The proposed error stage is precise: an extra observed **X** entered the
fractionated coordinate stream **before transposition**. After applying the
original 401-slot inverse transposition, excluding that X restores an even
400-coordinate paired stream. The received ciphertext is not shortened to
400 before inverse transposition.

Two adjacent interim X coordinates are indistinguishable under this model:

in the certificate; no unique physical origin is asserted. The excluded X

**Readable German — complete edited stream, with spacing and punctuation only:**

> Dtsche Militaermission in Konstantinopel. Zu Nr 45610. Komdo des Oberlts Siegfried nach der Tuerkei, dessen Zuteilung zur Militaermission ist aufgehoben. AKO folgt. Auf allerhoechsten Befehl Chef Militaerkabinetts Frhr Marschall.

**Meaning:** Regarding 45610 and the posting of Oberleutnant Siegfried to Turkey, the message cancels his assignment to the Military Mission. AKO follows. It closes with the Chief of the Military Cabinet formula and FRHR MARSCHALL. The message does not report a completed return journey or establish a separate biographical identity.

The original reference **45610** and the abbreviations **DTSCHE**, **KOMDO**,
**OBERLTS**, **AKO** and **FRHR** remain literal in the reading. No digit is
relabelled, and no abbreviation is expanded into an independently asserted
person or document.

## Exact forward witness and bounded stage comparison

To reconstruct the actual source shape:

1. Fractionate the proposed 200-character plaintext with the unchanged square.
2. Reinsert the observed surplus X at interim 230 or 231, restoring 401 coordinates.
3. Transpose those 401 coordinates with the unchanged column ranks.
4. Compare with the literal source: exactly seven observed substitutions and
   four dash fills remain, and no received source position has been deleted.

This replay succeeds for both aliases. Ordinary encryption of the 200-position
proposal into a 400-coordinate ciphertext is not presented as the source
witness; the declared surplus stage is part of the verified reconstruction.

All single-coordinate exclusions at both relevant stages were priced against
the same complete proposed text:

| Model stage | Candidates examined | Minimum observed substitutions | Minimum positions | Mask fills at minima | Observed surplus/excluded coordinates |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
|After inverse transposition of the 401-slot source|401|7|Interim 230 or 231|4|1|
|From the received ciphertext before inverse transposition|401|144|Source 297, 298 or 299|4|1|

The comparison favors the declared stage within this fixed-template,
single-coordinate family. It does not prove what happened historically,
exclude multi-error explanations, or turn the whole tail into a direct
ordinary-frame decoding.

## Independent support for the complete closing

The last **58 plaintext positions**, beginning `AUFALLERHOECHSTENBEFEHL`,
match the direct raw closing of [p149 A2](osthoff.md). That older physical
record has its own [source crop](../../experiments/corpus_attack/sources/childs_p149_A2.png),
which was reviewed again for this comparison. The new certificate checks
all 58 coordinate pairs, their letters and both sets of source positions.

This supports the repairs in `BEFEHL`, `MILITAERKABINETTS` and `FRHR` without
inventing a smoother signature from general historical expectations. The
parallel concerns Osthoff, so its other body words and identity are not
transferred to Siegfried's order. It is not counted as another newly recovered
record.
