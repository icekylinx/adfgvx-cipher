## Reading

**Readable German:**

> Nr. 607. Bitte dafuer zu sorgen, dass niederlaendischer Kurier,
> Rittmeister Vreede, der mit unserem COSPOLI-Kurier zusammen am 3.11.
> hier abfaehrt, in Braila mit dem Schiff nach Konstantinopel
> mitgenommen wird. Auswaertiges Amt. 1. 41362.

**Meaning:** The Foreign Office asks the Political Section to arrange for
the Dutch courier, Rittmeister Vreede, to be taken aboard the ship from
Braila to Constantinople. He is to leave the sender's location on
3 November together with its `COSPOLI` courier.

The surname **VREEDE**, rank, nationality, **607**, **3.11**, Braila,
Constantinople and closing **1 / 41362** all come directly from unchanged
source coordinates. The text does not identify Vreede by first name.
`COSPOLI` is retained as transmitted, and the source does not itself name
the location meant by “here.” Spacing, punctuation and capitalization are
editorial.
