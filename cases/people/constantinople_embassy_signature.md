## p162 D1

Childs printed p162 (PDF p165), D, has header `XYZ v LP`, receipt
`06.12 a.m., November 10th`, and the literal origin field
`1330 (8/13) 2 TEILE`. The unusual `8/13` is retained as printed, without
turning it into an assumed calendar date. The surviving body is labeled
`1 TL RICHI-100`; the other part is not identified here.

The [source crop](../../experiments/nov8_map_and_boundary/sources/p162_D_detail.png)
contains **100 slots**, including printed C glyphs at source 22, 37 and 79.
Those noncoordinate letters are kept in the literal transcript and each
represented by one mask in the analysis. The source does not acquire a
different length. It decodes under the unchanged published **Nov7–9** key,
although its receipt header is November 10.

## Scope and credit

This is an address/signature fragment. It does not establish the missing
substantive request, a particular person's identity or the association of
another nearby telegram merely because the name STUMM appears there.
The original part label and odd origin field remain visible.

The earlier OCR-derived candidate already identified the embassy/signature
wording. This source edition retains that earlier work and the published
key's attribution; it makes no first historical decipherment claim. The
[inputs](../../experiments/nov8_map_and_boundary/inputs.json),
[certificates](../../experiments/nov8_map_and_boundary/certificates.json)
and [reproduction guide](../../experiments/nov8_map_and_boundary/README.md)
