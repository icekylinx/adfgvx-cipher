# p210 B — Consul Burkhardt's oil-company assignment

**Record:** p210 B, 20 November 1918, to Consul Burkhardt in Tiflis.
**Result:** **158/158 raw plaintext positions** are fixed by the unchanged
published Nov19–21 key. Five explicit coordinate substitutions restore
body words; the date-like **J** and the administrative references remain
literal and historically unresolved.

## Readable body

**Readable German:**

> An Konsul Burkhardt, Tiflis. Ihr Auftrag fuer
> Kriegsschmieroelgesellschaft am J Dezember beendet.
> K.R.A. Nr. 455, JJ, J4, A. Nch.
> Kriegsministerium, Allgem. Kriegsdepartement.

**Meaning:** A War Ministry telegram informs Consul Burkhardt in Tiflis
that his assignment for the lubricating oil company is ended on a December
date. Its day is transmitted as **J** under the published key. This edition
does not silently turn that letter into a numeral or establish a specific
historical completion date.

The addressee and place are directly read. `KRA`, `A NCH`, **455**, **JJ**
and **J4** remain source strings without a forced administrative expansion
or date interpretation. The prose supplies only spacing, capitalization
and punctuation. It does not identify the consul beyond the printed surname.

All **316** printed coordinates are valid. The direct decipherment is:

**Raw output:**

```text
ANKONSULXBURKHARDTXTIFLISXIHREUFTRAGFUE3KRIEGSSCH1IEROELGESE8LJCHAFTAMXJXDEZEMBERBEENDETXKXRXAXXNRX455XJJXJ4XAXNCHXKRIEGSMINISTERIUMALLGEMXKRIEGSDEPARTEMENTXX
```

The same-length candidate changes only the body sequences
`IHREUFTRAG → IHRAUFTRAG`, `FUE3 → FUER`, and
`KRIEGSSCH1IEROELGESE8LJCHAFT → KRIEGSSCHMIEROELGESELLSCHAFT`.
The exact coordinate substitutions are:

| Source index, zero-based | Source | Proposed |
|---:|:---:|:---:|
| 2 | F | V |
| 3 | G | F |
| 4 | V | G |
| 211 | F | V |
| 306 | G | A |

The candidate preserves **311** source coordinates and re-encrypts exactly
after those five changes. No source erasure, length change, column-order
change or square relabelling is involved. The known raw `J` in the date
and the `J` strings in the footer are untouched.
