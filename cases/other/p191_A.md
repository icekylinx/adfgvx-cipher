# p191 A — Boots and clothing for Oderberg

**Status: body recovered with a conditional five-coordinate group move;
unit/quantity symbols and one connective remain unresolved.** The two
parts fix **196 of 200 plaintext positions** after four explicit
known-coordinate substitutions and twelve printed-dash fills. Their
lengths remain 214 and 186 coordinates, and the published key is unchanged.

The message asks a deputy corps command at Breslau to make boots and
heavy clothing available initially for Oderberg, on call and with advance
notice. A wire/radio reply is requested. The repaired signature is
`A.O.K.11`. The exact corps symbol and number of pairs of boots are not
supplied from historical plausibility.


Only two further observed-coordinate changes are needed in this part,
both `F → V` at original coordinates 127 and 130, plus eleven dash
fills. One source erasure remains at the connective.

The geometry check masks the entire footer and uses 77 ordinary-word
positions, with no names, unit numbers or quantities. Across the identity
and **all one-complete-group moves** among 37 complete groups—1,333
operations, 1,297 distinct streams—the displayed move is the sole
zero-disagreement candidate. The untouched source has nineteen
ordinary-word coordinate disagreements. A fresh control with the same
length, twelve erased positions and group displacement likewise recovers
its true order as the unique zero-disagreement candidate.

The word mask was developed after inspecting the damaged source, so
this is a conditional consistency check, not a blind language experiment
or a proof that no other error mechanism is possible. The certificate
retains all minimizing operations, not a chosen tie.
