# Naval reporting after dissolution of the Seekriegsleitung

[ADFGVX Cipher](../../README.md) · [All readings](../README.md) · [Source and verification](../../experiments/corpus_joint/README.md)

**Record:** p205 B, two parts, intercepted on 17 November 1918 with the internal date `16/11`. **Result:** the unchanged Nov16–18 partial key fixes **269 of 271 plaintext positions** from all **542 printed coordinates**. The order ends submission of the daily reports and war diaries to the dissolved command, while requiring offices to continue keeping their diaries.

## Most likely reading

> Mit Auflösung der Seekriegsleitung fallen die bisher dahin erstatteten Tagesmeldungen fort. Das gleiche gilt für Vorlage der Kriegstagebücher. Kriegstagebücher [s]ind von allen Dienststellen vorläufig weiterzuführen. Gleichlautend Ost-, Nordflotte, BMK. Admiralstab 1… Op. Eins.

**English meaning:** With dissolution of the Naval Warfare Command, the daily reports previously sent there are discontinued. The same applies to submission of the war diaries. Every office is to continue maintaining its war diary for the time being. The closing names further addressees and gives an Admiral Staff reference whose second digit remains unknown.

Punctuation, word spacing and the separation of the compact addressee string are editorial. The repeated `KRIEGSTAGEBUECHER` is meaningful: one sentence concerns submitting the diaries, and the next concerns continuing to maintain them. It is therefore retained. `BMK` is not expanded to a specific office on the strength of this ciphertext alone.

## Direct reading and one explicit emendation

```text
MITXAUFLOESUNGXDERXSEEKRIEGSLEITUNGXFALLENXDIEXBISHERXDAHINXERSTATTETENXTAGESMELDUNGENXFORTXDASGLEICHEXGILTXFUERXVORLAGEXDERXKRIEGSTAGEBUECHERXKRIEGSTAGEBUECHER
?INDXVONXALLENXDIENSTSTELLENXVORLAEUFIGXWEITERZUFUEHRENXGLEICHLAUTENDXOSTNORDFLOTTEXBMKXADMIRALSTABX1?XOPXEINSX
```

Part B1's **160/160 positions** are fixed. Part B2 fixes **109/111**. Its initial unknown is the square cell `GD`, whose possible values under the published partial square are `03456789`. Consequently, `SIND` cannot be obtained by completing an unassigned square cell: `S` is already established at `GA`. The proposed initial `S` requires the single source-coordinate change **B2 index 128 `D → A`**. All other 221 B2 coordinates remain unchanged, and the final unknown digit is left unresolved.

This is a readable editorial reconstruction with an exact one-coordinate cost, not a complete unchanged-source decipherment. The [certificate](../../experiments/corpus_joint/p205_readings.json) supplies the two raw texts, all plaintext domains and the edit map.

## Source and credit

The [p205 B image](../../experiments/corpus_joint/sources/p205_B.png) contains 320 coordinates in B1 and 222 in B2. The extra internal space in B1's printed `V AAFX` does not remove a coordinate. The B2 groups `GXXAV`, `FGAXA` and `DDXDV` were checked at higher magnification; their letters are preserved in the source transcription even where the result suggests a copying error.

The historical source is J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, printed p. 205, PDF p. 208 in the [complete scan](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view). The header reads `ASO v LP (6.38 a.m., November 17th)`, `FUER ASO 1655 (16/11) 2 TEILE B M K SEBASTOPOL`.

The **Nov16–18** period key retains the attribution in [data/keys.json](../../data/keys.json) to **George Lasry** and the historical/research work of **George Lasry, Ingo Niebel, Nils Kopal and Arno Wacker**. Source assembly, review, exact edit costs and this edition are by **icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro**. No first historical decipherment priority is claimed.
