# p210 D — request for the situation in Reval

[ADFGVX Cipher](../../README.md) · [All readings](../README.md) · [Source certificate](../../experiments/corpus_recovery/certificates.json)

**Record:** p210 D, 20 November 1918. **Result:** all **58 plaintext
positions** are fixed from **116 unchanged source coordinates** under the
published Nov19–21 key. One explicitly costed spelling repair gives:

> Bitte um Mitteilung, wie Lage in Reval. Kriegsministerium Berlin.

**Meaning:** The War Ministry in Berlin asks for a report on the situation
in Reval. The telegram gives no further description of the situation.

## Source and one repair

The [source image](../../experiments/corpus_recovery/sources/p210_D.png)
preserves the header:

```text
KOW v LP (05.55 p.m., November 20th)
FUER RVA 22.00 (20/11)
RICHI-116
```

Its final groups are **`XXDDV V`**. An earlier OCR reading had an extra
terminal `V` and an uncertain glyph; both are corrected by reading the
source, with no historical-coordinate deletion or invented fill.

The literal decipherment is:

```text
BITTEUMMITTEILUNCXWIELAGEINXREVALXKRIEGSMINISTERIUMBERLINX
```

The proposed **MITTEILUNC → MITTEILUNG** changes source index **6: A→G**.
The other **115** coordinates are unchanged. The candidate retains the
telegram's abbreviated grammar and re-encrypts exactly after that single
substitution. Spacing, capitalization and punctuation are editorial.
