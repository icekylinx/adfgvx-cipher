## Independent historical context

[Hopman's contemporary diary](../data/reviews/hopman_context.json), published in an edited primary-source volume, records Canterbury at Sevastopol on November 24 and the Allied squadron on November 26. This independently corroborates the existing Prinz/Horvat interpretation alongside the British ship logs. It does not authenticate the separately reported key manuscript.

The Australian War Memorial also preserves a [24 November fleet memorandum](https://transcribe.awm.gov.au/transcription/item/rcdig0000179-4)
recording the advance ships' departure on 23 November and the main fleet's
planned departure from Constantinople on 25 November. The
[source review](../experiments/p215_context/README.md#what-the-documents-support)
checks the original images and distinguishes these departure orders from the
diary's arrival record.
