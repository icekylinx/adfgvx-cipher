# p153b — Rearguard artillery ammunition report

[All cases](README.md) · [ADFGVX Cipher](../README.md)

**Recovery and review: icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro, 2 November 2026.** The shared
key recovered with [part 5](p153a.md) fixes **33 of 54 raw positions** using
nineteen ordinary-letter square cells. Fifteen source coordinates are erased.

## Most likely reading

```text
MUNITIONSBESTAND DER NACHHUTARTILLERIE MELDEN
X <gamma> X G X R <u><v><w> B X A
```

“Report the ammunition inventory of the rearguard artillery.” The trailing
initials or fragments are not identified. This full template is conditional
on the body repairs below and on interpreting square cell `AF` as the `X`
separator. It fixes **50 of 54 positions** within that model; the untouched
nineteen-letter square still fixes only **33 of 54**.

The `<gamma>` is the same unresolved character as both outer characters of
the serial in [part 5](p153a.md). The three erased footer positions are coupled:
`u=v` or `v=w` can occur only when both are `G`; `u=w` is otherwise possible.
Even after choosing a date/serial assignment, **2,537** footer triples remain
possible. No signature, unit, numeral or padding convention is selected. See
the [exact linked-field analysis](../experiments/p153_extension/README.md).

## Untouched source and explicit repairs

The conservative raw decode is:

```text
M?N?T?ONSB?S?A?DDERNA?H?UTARTI?G?RIEMEL??????G?R???B?A
```

The proposed first 42 positions are a **conditional reconstruction**, requiring twelve erased-coordinate fills and
two explicit changes to observed coordinates:

| Source index, zero based | Printed | Proposed | Effect |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 3 | A | X | Raw `G` becomes `L` in `ARTILLERIE` |
| 4 | A | X | Unassigned `AA` cell becomes `E` in `MELDEN` |

The source clearly prints both A characters. They remain unchanged in the
catalog. The [certificate](../data/certificates/p153.json) lists every fill,
coordinate mapping and disagreement: seventy known coordinates constrained by
the proposed body agree, two disagree, and twelve are absent. Three further
erasures lie in the unresolved twelve-character trailing text.

Interpreting square cell `AF` as an `X` separator adds four readable separator
positions, but is kept separate from the nineteen-letter core. No numeral,
signature, unit or remaining text is invented.

## Source

Printed page 153 / PDF page 156, section C6; November 8, 1918.
`FKR v CGH (11.55 p.m.); 2250; 6 TL; CHI-108`. The header means CGH to FKR.
Parts 5 and 6 survive; the source does not state a total number of parts.

## Restricted validation and credit

The [exact crib check](../experiments/p153_crib/README.md) exhausts the width-18
keys compatible with the sixty-letter part-5 reading. Its only two partial-key
representations are equivalent, and both require exactly the two changes above
for this second-part body. A different transposition within that model cannot
remove the discrepancies. This does not determine the historical cause or
prove the proposed wording independently of its linguistic reading.

unsuccessful searches and scoring failures remain in the experiment record.
