# p153b — Rearguard artillery ammunition report

[All cases](README.md) · [ADFGVX Cipher](../README.md) · [Reading conventions](../docs/reading-conventions.md)

The shared key recovered with [part 5](p153a.md) supports an instruction to report the rearguard artillery's ammunition stock. Its nineteen established letter cells fix **33 of 54 raw positions**; the readable reconstruction below requires twelve erased-coordinate fills and two source corrections.

## Most likely reading

**Readable German — conditional on the source repairs and separator interpretation below:**

> Munitionsbestand der Nachhutartillerie melden.

The twelve-position closing remains unidentified. The full template preserves its known labels and linked unknown cells:

**Conditional interpretation — readable reconstruction with AF = X and linked unresolved footer variables:**

```text
MUNITIONSBESTAND DER NACHHUTARTILLERIE MELDEN
X <gamma> X G X R <u><v><w> B X A
```

**Meaning:** Report the ammunition inventory of the rearguard artillery. With the stated body repairs and square cell `AF` interpreted as the separator `X`, the full template fixes **50 of 54 positions**.

The `<gamma>` is the same unresolved character as both outer characters of
the serial in [part 5](p153a.md). The three erased footer positions are coupled:
`u=v` or `v=w` can occur only when both are `G`; `u=w` is otherwise possible.
Even after a date/serial assignment, **2,537** footer triples remain possible. The [linked-field analysis](../experiments/p153_extension/README.md) gives their exact constraints.

The proposed first 42 positions are a **conditional reconstruction**, requiring twelve erased-coordinate fills and
two explicit changes to observed coordinates:

| Source index, zero based | Printed | Proposed | Effect |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 3 | A | X | Raw `G` becomes `L` in `ARTILLERIE` |
| 4 | A | X | Unassigned `AA` cell becomes `E` in `MELDEN` |

Both source glyphs are clearly printed A. Of the coordinates constrained by the proposed body, seventy agree, two require the changes above, and twelve are erased. The remaining three erasures lie in the closing text. The [certificate](../data/certificates/p153.json) records every origin and fill; the separate `AF=X` interpretation supplies four separator positions.

## Source

Printed page 153 / PDF page 156, section C6; November 8, 1918.
`FKR v CGH (11.55 p.m.); 2250; 6 TL; CHI-108`. The header means CGH to FKR.
Parts 5 and 6 survive; the source does not state a total number of parts.
