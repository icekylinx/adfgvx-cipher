## Most likely reading

> […] aufnahmefaehig. Nikolajew auf Wunsch Ex. Kiew bestimmt. Hptm. Joel, V. O. FECH, Odessa: [welche Schiffe] etwa wegen Eisenbahnlage nach Nikolajew zugehen haben. […]

**Meaning:** A continuation concerning port capacity and which ships should be directed to Nikolayev in view of the railway situation. Captain Joel and a reference to the railway organization in Odessa appear in the text.

**Notes:** The initial clause is a continuation, not a self-contained sentence. `Hptm.` abbreviates captain; `Ex. Kiew` and `V. O. FECH` are retained without forcing a unique expansion. The bracketed phrase is editorial: three plaintext positions remain unresolved in the conservative source layer.
