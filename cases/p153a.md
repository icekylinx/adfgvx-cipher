## Relationship to part 6

This is **part 5** of transmission `2250`, intercepted on November 8 at 23:55. [p153b](p153b.md) is part 6 of the same transmission. Parts 1–4 were not identified in the inspected book corpus. Reusing the known November 13 CHI key on November 8 is a secondary hypothesis, not a source-established constraint.

## Attack status

The [joint search](../experiments/joint_search/README.md) retains all 132 observed coordinates here and the 93 observed coordinates plus 15 erasures in part 6. It exhausts the length-class segmentations at specified widths, then uses bounded pairing/order/square searches. Controls show both successful recovery and serious ranking failures; no historical reading is accepted.

[Separate direct checks](../experiments/geometry/README.md) test concatenating both parts into one 240-coordinate rectangle and continuing the writing-column phase between parts. These are distinct from the usual reset-per-part model. They do not exhaust arbitrary unknown keys or a longer original rectangle with earlier parts absent.
