## Most likely reading

> Funkstelle Kertsch erhaelt ab 12.[11] Rufnamen Richard Emil Karl. Funkstellen dortigen Bereichs benachrichtigen. Nachrichtenchef 4BG7834.

**Meaning:** The Kerch radio station is to use the call sign spelled out as Richard–Emil–Karl from the stated date, most plausibly 12 November. Notify the radio stations in that area. The closing administrative reference remains literal.

**Notes:** The revised repair deletes one of two identical printed `XAAXV` groups and inserts five missing coordinates at the earlier proposed gap. It preserves **all 239 retained source coordinates without substitution**. The proposed fill `AAXAA` gives the readable text above; its five supplied letters are `L, 1, N, E, F`. The remaining month character is `1` if `12.1?` is interpreted as an ordinary day.month date. The missing group is not visible in the source, so the geometry and completion remain explicit hypotheses.

This fixes **117 of 122 positions**. The five remaining domains permit 7,776 strings; the most likely completion is selected by the words and date convention. The final administrative reference is not expanded.
