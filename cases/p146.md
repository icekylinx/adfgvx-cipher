# p146 — Kerch radio call-sign instruction

[Case index](README.md) · [ADFGVX Cipher](../README.md) · [Reading conventions](../docs/reading-conventions.md)

The Kerch radio station is assigned the call sign Richard–Emil–Karl. Our duplicate-group reconstruction of the Nov4–6-key message fixes **117 of 122 positions** while retaining all 239 coordinates outside the duplicated group unchanged.

## Most likely reading

**Readable German — reconstructed frame and word repairs stated below:**

> Funkstelle Kertsch erhaelt ab 12.[11] Rufnamen Richard Emil Karl. Funkstellen dortigen Bereichs benachrichtigen. Nachrichtenchef 4BG7834.

**Meaning:** The Kerch radio station is to use the call sign spelled out as Richard–Emil–Karl from the stated date, most plausibly 12 November. Notify the radio stations in that area. The closing administrative reference remains literal.

The conditional model removes one of two identical `XAAXV` groups and restores a missing five-coordinate group. The proposed fill `AAXAA` supplies `L, 1, N, E, F` in the reading. Its month digit is `1` under the ordinary day.month interpretation of `12.1?`.
