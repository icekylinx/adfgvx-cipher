## Most likely reading

> Germania-[Etappe] Konstantinopel. Fuer [Mittelmeerdivision]. Zu [Tel. Nr.] 62, [Div.-Telegr. Nr.] 58: […] 32141 Q4 A vom 4. November [erledigt]. [Admiralstab]. 32398 B.

**Meaning:** A message for the Mediterranean naval division, routed through the Germania staging organization in Constantinople, reports that a matter in earlier telegrams has been dealt with. The intervening reference wording is damaged.

**Notes:** Brackets mark editorial interpretations of garbled words. The reference numbers are retained without attempting to reconstruct their administrative meaning. Baertl's longer completion is printed below for attribution; its extra wording is not recovered by the 143-position model. ## Explicit repair of the orphan coordinate

The unpaired final intermediate `D` comes from original source index **244** (the 245th received character, final `D` of group 49 `XAXGD`). Deleting it in addition to the three published deletions produces 286 coordinates and exactly the same 143-position body, now with a complete ordinary re-encryption. This equivalent reconstruction is a contribution of the present arithmetic review; it does not establish the historical error mechanism.

A separate [optional two-deletion model](../experiments/p105_optional_two_deletions/README.md) retains 288 of the 290 printed coordinates and gives a fully paired 144-label raw stream, with three explicitly priced ordinary-word substitutions. It preserves the earlier reading above and documents all twelve equal-cost word-only deletion descriptions, retrospective held-out comparisons and independent native vocabulary witnesses. Its changed reference/date labels remain geometry-dependent alternatives, and the visibly printed excluded coordinates remain in its full source ledger.
