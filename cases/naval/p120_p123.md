# p120 / p123 - Three-part instruction to Feigenhaus

[ADFGVX Cipher](../../README.md) · [All readings](../README.md) · [Source records](../../experiments/corpus_readings/selected_sources.json) · [Reading conventions](../../docs/reading-conventions.md)

**Records:** p120 B1, the repeated suffix p122 D, and p123 A2-A3.
**Result:** an assembled three-part message under the published Nov4-6 key.
The 461-position reading below is conditional on the repairs in the table:
five valid-coordinate substitutions, nineteen erasure fills and one deletion.
The names and unusual expressions remain literal; their intended referents
have not been established.

**Prior body reading:** George Lasry, Ingo Niebel, Nils Kopal and Arno Wacker
already describe the three-part Feigenhaus/Herkules message in §6.5 of
[*Deciphering ADFGVX messages from the Eastern Front of World War I*](https://doi.org/10.1080/01611194.2016.1169461),
printed p24 / author-PDF p25. The present contribution by **icekylinx and
GPT-6 Astra Pro** is the source-level edition, exact repair accounting and
identification of the p122 D repeated suffix. This includes the inherited
prior body of that suffix; it is not a first decipherment of the message.

## Most likely reading

**Readable German:**

> Feigenhaus, Konstantinopel. Sechshundertsiebzehn. Beichtvater
> zweihundertdreiundneunzig. Nachdem Diktator Anweisung gegeben,
> Notschreie in Andreas nicht vorzunehmen, haben wir uns nochmals mit
> Herkules in Verbindung gesetzt.
>
> Herkules ablehnt Verantwortung fuer neue Notschreie. Infolgedessen
> koennen wir auf keinen Fall neue Notschreie in Andreas vornehmen.
>
> Sie wollen deshalb unseren Anweisungen entsprechend sich verhalten.
> Drahtet, was ihr in Anbetracht veraenderter politischer Lage nunmehr
> zu tun gedenkt. Reichsversorgung.

**Meaning:** The sender reports renewed contact with Herkules after Diktator
instructed that no further *Notschreie* be undertaken in Andreas. Herkules
declines responsibility, so the prohibition stands. The recipient is to follow
these instructions and telegraph the action now intended in view of the
changed political situation. The opening names Feigenhaus in Constantinople
and the references 617 and 293, written out as words in the ciphertext.

*Notschreie* literally means cries for help. Together with Feigenhaus,
Beichtvater, Diktator, Andreas, Herkules and Reichsversorgung, it may belong
to a special vocabulary. This edition does not substitute guessed people,
places, institutions or operations for these terms. The irregular telegram
word order and the shift from *Sie* to *ihr* are retained. Word spacing,
punctuation and capitalization are editorial.

## Assembly, dates and credit

The source labels the parts `1 TL`, `2 TL`, `3 TL` and `3 TEILE`.
The p120 header is `OSM v OUI (8.32 p.m., November 4th); 1956`;
p122 repeats its suffix at 8.33 p.m. with `1056`, and p123 supplies parts 2
and 3 at 8.40 p.m., also with `1056`. The differing four-digit fields are
retained. The route, part labels and exact repeated suffix support the
assembly. No date is changed to fit the period key.

The source is J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher
System*, printed pp. 120, 122-123 / PDF pp. 123, 125-126 in the
[public scan](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view).
The complete Nov4-6 key retains its attribution to George Lasry and the
historical/research sources in [the key register](../../data/keys.json).
