# p210 A — Helsinki and return transport for the Ostsee Division

[ADFGVX Cipher](../../README.md) · [All readings](../README.md) · [Source certificate](../../experiments/corpus_recovery/certificates.json) · [Reading conventions](../../docs/reading-conventions.md)

**Record:** the two parts of p210 A, intercepted on 20 November 1918.
**Result:** all **208 plaintext positions** are fixed from **416 source
coordinates** under the published Nov19–21 key. Four explicit coordinate
substitutions give the following readable reconstruction.

## Reading

**Readable German:**

> Fustation Helsingfors mitteilet hierher, ob Ruecktransportmoeglichkeit
> fuer Ostseedivision gesichert.
>
> Andernfalls wird versucht, Schiffe dorthin zu entsenden. Welcher
> Verladehafen kaeme in Frage? Zentralfunkleitung, Vollzugsrat Berlin.

**Meaning:** The radio station at Helsingfors is asked to report whether
return transport for the Ostsee Division is secured. If it is not, the
sender will try to send ships there and asks which embarkation port would
be suitable. The closing names the Central Radio Direction and the
Executive Council in Berlin.

The unusual **MITTEILET** is retained as a same-length reading; it is not
silently shortened or replaced with a longer modern expression. The names
Helsingfors, Ostseedivision and Berlin are directly deciphered. The source
does not establish whether the proposed shipping was subsequently arranged.
Word spacing, punctuation and capitalization are editorial.
