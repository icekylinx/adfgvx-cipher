## Most likely reading

**Readable German — earlier credited reconstruction:**

> In Ukraine und Polen Rubel[kurse stark] steigend infolge Bruches zwischen Deutschland und Sowjetregierung und Erwartung der Wiederherstellung Russlands durch Deutschland und Entente.

**Meaning:** The ruble exchange rate is rising sharply in Ukraine and Poland because of the break between Germany and the Soviet government and expectations that Germany and the Entente will restore Russia.

**Notes:** The passage reports a contemporary expectation; it is not a statement that the expected restoration occurred. Baertl's natural `KURSE STARK` adds an `S` to the exact raw `KURSETARK`. All other wording shown here follows the reproduced body, with spaces and punctuation supplied.
