## Most likely reading

**Readable German:**

> [Unloesbare Funksprueche] nach Dezemberschluessel entziffern. Irrtum.

**Meaning:** “Decipher unreadable radio messages using the December key.”
The separate closing word `IRRTUM` means “error” or “mistake.” Its precise
function in this transmission is not established. Word spacing, punctuation
and capitalization are editorial.

The two bracketed words need **two known-coordinate substitutions**, with no
erasure fills or changes to the key. The rest of the proposed text is directly
decoded. In particular, **DEZEMBER and IRRTUM are source-supported** despite the
printed **15 November 1918** date. The edition does not replace December with
November, correct the date, or infer a cancellation procedure.
