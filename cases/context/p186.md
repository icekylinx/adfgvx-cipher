## Most likely reading

**Readable German — body interpretation; the final ellipsis omits the unresolved administrative ending shown in Part 2:**

> Russischen und poln. Heereverkehr voll erfassen. Wichtiges, besond. aus poln.
> Verkehr, ueber OHL-Station verziffert funken. Soweit Fernschreiber-Verbdg.
> nicht arbeitet, Rest schriftlich. [Nachchef] […]

**Meaning:** Monitor Russian and Polish army traffic comprehensively. Radio
important material, especially Polish traffic, in cipher through the Supreme
Army Command station. Where the teleprinter connection is not working, send
the rest in writing. The administrative ending remains incomplete.

Word divisions and punctuation are editorial. Part 1's readable completion
uses three erasure fills supported by corresponding words in [p187](../p187.md).
The source's abbreviated `BESOND` and `HEEREVERKEHR` are retained here; this
transmission is not identical to p187/p187b.
