Asgard is expected at Poti to collect troops. The same message reports that
Asgard and Tresivolus arrived at the sender's port on 7 November and asks for
acknowledgment. The port is not named in the ciphertext. Hopman's diary
places the two ships at Sevastopol on that date, with orders to return to
Poti; its spelling *Tresivolos* differs from the telegram's `TRESIVOLUS`.
[Diary passages and source references](../../data/reviews/hopman_context.json).

## Consequence for p73

This message follows the same radio route as p73 and was received five
minutes later, but the origin dates differ. p73 has an October 26 heading
and `0911 (26)`; p155 has `2035 (7)`. Reception-time proximity therefore
does not establish a common original-date key. The [p73 source-context record](../../data/reviews/p73_source_context.json)
retains the dating evidence and the unsuccessful search for an October 25–27
companion.
