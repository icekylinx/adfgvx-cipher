## Most likely reading

**Readable German — conditional reconstructed body; the source-frame assumption remains explicit:**

> Dampfer Asgard eintrifft Poti Poti [voraussichtlich] in naechsten Tagen, um dort [befindliche] Truppen [abzuholen]. Asgard Asgard, Tresivolus Tresivolus, 7.11. hier eingelaufen. [Empfang bestaetigen]. BQZ K.

**Meaning:** The steamer Asgard is expected at Poti in the next few days to pick up the troops there. Asgard and Tresivolus arrived at the sender's port on 7 November. Acknowledge receipt.

**Notes:** Repeated names are retained from the telegram. Brackets mark readable normalizations of damaged words. The second part depends on the stated missing-coordinate geometry; its choice is not unique. The independent diary spells the second ship *Tresivolos*, whereas this ciphertext reads `TRESIVOLUS` twice. The origin port is not named in the deciphered sentence; the Sevastopol context comes from the diary, not an inserted plaintext word.

The repeated Asgard in the readable passage that corresponds to `A?GARD` is a completion of one unresolved position supported by another occurrence within the same message. It is not a fixed raw letter or an independently observed coordinate. All four conditional source frames remain in the certificate.

The [source](../../sources/excerpts/childs_p155_A.png) gives `POT v NKJ`, onward to `COS GENERAL V KRESS`, clear origin `2035 (7)`, intercepted on November 8 at 02:28. This is the same radio route as p73, received five minutes earlier, but a different stated origin day.
