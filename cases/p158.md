## Earlier historical reading of a component

Childs's report dated **20 November 1918**, printed on p42 of *German Military Ciphers from February to November 1918* (1935), deciphers order **11338**. Its SY→RVV via N4 header, 9 November date and 21:03 time agree with the p168 retransmission. [Primary report, p42](https://www.nsa.gov/Portals/75/documents/news-features/declassified-documents/friedman-documents/publications/FOLDER_268/41784789082381.pdf).

The report expands the garbled destination as `LANDWEHR REGIMENT; AN FECH`. The coordinate-derived `LA3DSBERGXAN0LECH` more closely suggests Landsberg an/am Lech; the exact expansion remains editorial. The surrounding report describes a separate **double-transposition army cipher**. It does not establish two complete ADFGVX encipherments.

The component bodies on **p168 C and D** are also discussed by **George Lasry,
Ingo Niebel, Nils Kopal and Arno Wacker** in §6.4 of
[*Deciphering ADFGVX messages from the Eastern Front of World War I*](https://doi.org/10.1080/01611194.2016.1169461),
printed p23 / author-PDF p24. That credit also applies to the parallel p159 B/C bodies. Their gloss describes doubled confirmation; the raw text distinguishes the doubled army-key instruction from receipt acknowledgment.

The p158 composite identification and repair audit are by **icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro, 2 November 2026**. The [historical comparison](../data/reviews/historical_component_prior.json) records the earlier readings.

The [full p168 C witness](radio/p168_munich_routing_witness.md) resolves the six-coordinate/four-coordinate group alignment. Its conditional frame fixes 139/140 raw positions, and an observed `V` from p159 B supplies the last coordinate. Twelve ownership aliases yield the same completed stream. The observed p168 pair `VG = K` also supports `GEN KDO`: adopting its G at p159 source 169 costs one `X→G` substitution, changing the H in `GEN HDO` above.
