## Length qualification

The header advertises **CHI-170**, while the body contains **165 valid coordinates**. No erased group is explicitly printed. A single five-coordinate loss is a plausible model, but the source does not establish its location or contiguity. Ordinary complete ADFGVX requires an even coordinate count; silent truncation is not accepted.

This challenge target is **section D**. The separately readable
[p198 C transport message](context/transport.md) and the newly linked
[p198 B/E equipment and lorry orders](context/transport_197_198.md) use the
`RIZI` identifier and do not solve this `CHI` entry. Section E contains one
extra five-coordinate group, but moving that group into D is only a tested
corruption hypothesis, not an established source repair.

### Exact scattered-loss experiments

The [marginal decoder](../experiments/p198_exact_sparse/README.md) covers all
**1,115,034,284** possible positions of five scattered losses for each supplied
complete key. The [joint decoder](../experiments/p198_joint_exact/README.md)
also chooses their six-valued coordinates consistently, covering
**8,670,506,592,384 loss-and-fill witnesses**. The latter count includes aliases;
it does not count distinct plaintexts.

The recorded historical runs fix one transposition and one heuristic
completion of each of two partial squares. Neither gives a readable result.
The joint four-gram objective also selects wrong plaintexts in both synthetic
controls even when supplied with the true complete keys. The source witnesses,
exact scores and failures are retained. These experiments resolve the search
optimum under their stated assumptions; they do not establish a historical key,
validate the damage model, or solve p198.

The [six-gram calibration](../experiments/p198_joint_six/README.md) does recover
one complete 85-letter control, including its exact loss mask and fills, under
both pure-six and mixed objectives. A feasible wrong competitor still beats
the other control's truth under both objectives. This improves the demonstrated
capability of the method while preserving its remaining scoring limitation.

An independent [letter-count bound](../experiments/p198_letter_bounds/README.md)
covers **all 40,320 completions** of the partial Nov13–15b square, with its
recorded transposition and every five-loss placement/fill. The attainable
ordinary-letter counts range from 44 to 82, so this family requires at least
three digits among the 85 plaintext positions. This is a modest exact
constraint without a language model; the actual message's digit count is
unknown, so it does not reject the key.

The [direct source-similarity search](../experiments/source_similarity/README.md)
also checks all 788 extracted OCR blocks. Its leading p198 candidate is p154 A2;
independent page inspection confirms only a short local resemblance and does
not establish a parallel transmission.
