## Length qualification

The header advertises **CHI-170**, while the body contains **165 valid coordinates**. No erased group is explicitly printed. A single five-coordinate loss is a plausible model, but the source does not establish its location or contiguity. Ordinary complete ADFGVX requires an even coordinate count; silent truncation is not accepted.

This challenge target is **section D**. The separately readable
[p198 C transport message](context/transport.md) and the newly linked
[p198 B/E equipment and lorry orders](context/transport_197_198.md) use the
`RIZI` identifier and do not solve this `CHI` entry. Section E contains one
extra five-coordinate group, but moving that group into D is only a tested
corruption hypothesis, not an established source repair.

## Attack status

### Exact scattered-loss experiments

The [marginal decoder](../experiments/p198_exact_sparse/README.md) covers all
**1,115,034,284** possible positions of five scattered losses for each supplied
complete key. The [joint decoder](../experiments/p198_joint_exact/README.md)
also chooses their six-valued coordinates consistently, covering
**8,670,506,592,384 loss-and-fill witnesses**. The latter count includes aliases;
it does not count distinct plaintexts.

The recorded historical runs fix one transposition and one heuristic
completion of each of two partial squares. Neither gives a readable result.
The joint four-gram objective also selects wrong plaintexts in both synthetic
controls even when supplied with the true complete keys. The source witnesses,
exact scores and failures are retained. These experiments resolve the search
optimum under their stated assumptions; they do not establish a historical key,
validate the damage model, or solve p198.

The [six-gram calibration](../experiments/p198_joint_six/README.md) does recover
one complete 85-position control, including its exact loss mask and fills, under
both pure-six and mixed objectives. A feasible wrong competitor still beats
the other control's truth under both objectives. This improves the demonstrated
capability of the method while preserving its remaining scoring limitation.

The subsequent [historical pure-six run](../experiments/p198_six_historical/README.md)
under the frozen CHI13 completion changes the best geometry and improves its
six-gram objective, but the full result remains unreadable. At that selected
geometry, the conservative partial square has 48 singleton positions; this is
a conditional diagnostic, not 48 newly recovered plaintext positions. The report keeps
the entire model output and its disagreements with four-gram ranking.

### Exact constraints without a language model

An independent [letter-count bound](../experiments/p198_letter_bounds/README.md)
covers **all 40,320 completions** of the partial Nov13–15b square, with its
recorded transposition and every five-loss placement/fill. The attainable
ordinary-letter counts range from 44 to 82, so this family requires at least
three digits among the 85 plaintext positions. This is a modest exact
constraint without a language model; the actual message's digit count is
unknown, so it does not reject the key.

A stronger [category audit](../experiments/p198_letter_bounds/CATEGORY_BOUNDS.md)
gives `digits + Q + Y ≥ 10` throughout the same partial-key family. If a proposed
plaintext contains no Q or Y, it must therefore contain at least ten digits;
a witness attains that bound. A separate Python algorithm factors the problem
into adjacent-column pairs and fully recomputes the optimum, independently of
the original C++ decoder. Neither inequality assumes that the historical text
had a particular digit or Q/Y count.

The complete [digit/Q/Y frontier](../experiments/p198_category_frontier/README.md)
sharpens those separate inequalities. The following count pairs are attainable,
and every other attainable pair has at least as many digits and Q/Y positions
as one of these:

| Digit positions | Minimum Q/Y positions at that digit count |
|---:|---:|
| 3 | 13 |
| 4 | 6 |
| 5 | 5 |
| 6 | 4 |
| 7 | 3 |
| 8 | 2 |
| 9 | 1 |
| 10 | 0 |

Thus **three digits force at least thirteen Q/Y occurrences**, rather than
the seven allowed by the separate inequalities alone. Every frontier point
has a complete source-preserving witness. Two separate algorithms exhaust
all 28 letter/digit membership classes and their 15,504 loss allocations;
285,264 short exhaustive witnesses include an explicit control with two
incomparable count pairs. Different points may require different square
completions. These exact counts constrain future proposals within this
family; they do not supply a readable historical message or reject the key.

### Conditional restriction from an independently attested name

The [Tiflis appointment](other/tiflis_appointment.md) supplies a separate
historical-name argument for **Nov13–15b FX = Y**. If that inference and the
Nov13b/five-loss hypothesis for p198 are both accepted, the exact family
shrinks to 5,040 complete squares. Its digit/QY frontier is
`(4,6), (5,5), (6,4), (7,3), (8,2), (10,1), (13,0)`.
Thus at least four digits are required, or at least thirteen if the reading
contains no Q or Y. The [conditional certificate](../experiments/contextual_key_constraints/README.md)
preserves all received coordinates, supplies witnesses for every frontier
point, and independently recomputes all seven retained membership classes.
Neither the historical counts nor this key's applicability to p198 are
established. No plaintext position becomes fixed from this count result.

### Why individual positions remain open

For either frozen complete-key hypothesis, **each of the 85 positions can
contain any of the 36 alphabet symbols in some compatible five-loss witness**.
The construction marks that position's two ciphertext coordinates as lost,
puts the remaining three losses elsewhere, and fills the selected pair with
the desired character. Every received coordinate still appears unchanged and
in order.

The [constructive domain audit](../experiments/p198_letter_bounds/domain_witnesses.json)
checks all **6,120** key/position/symbol witnesses and exact re-encryption.
Each choice can use a different witness; arbitrary choices at all positions
need not coexist in one plaintext. That is why full individual domains can
coexist with the joint requirement of at least three digits. Additional source
evidence about the missing positions could narrow those domains.

```sh
python3 experiments/p198_letter_bounds/domain_witnesses.py --verify
```
