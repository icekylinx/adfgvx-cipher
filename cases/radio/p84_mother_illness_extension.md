**Recovery and review: icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro, 4 November 2026.**
An additional conditional clause fits the interrupted p84 C reception:
**Mutter schwer krank. Bitte komm.** The combined conditional proposals assign **52/122
positions**, including the previously proposed *Deutsche Feldpost*, while
retaining every observed coordinate. The unchanged key and raw source frame
still fix **14/122** positions. Seventy positions remain unassigned under these proposals.

## Conditional reading

**Readable German — conditional lexical proposal; the surrounding passages remain unresolved:**

> […] Deutsche Feldpost […] [Mutter schwer krank. Bitte komm.] […]

**Meaning under this proposal:** A mother is seriously ill, and the recipient
is asked to come. The field-post fragment supplies a plausible setting for a
personal telegram. The relationship, destination and identities are not
established by the surviving text.

**Readable German — an equally compatible lexical counterexample:**

> […] Deutsche Feldpost […] [Mittelschwer krank. Bitte komm.] […]

This alternative means approximately “moderately ill; please come.” It changes
`MUTTER` to `MITTEL`, while retaining exactly the same coordinate prices in all
five frames. At position **69**, `U` and `I` share the observed half-coordinate;
position **73**, proposed as `R` or `L`, is wholly absent. The family-message
interpretation is a contextual preference, not a unique recovery of `MUTTER`.

## Discovery, verification and limits

A scan of the unchanged half-coordinate domains located the independent-book
words `MUTTER` or `MITTEL` at 68, `SCHWER` at 74, `KRANK` at 80 and `BITTE`
at 85. Examining the other retained frames then permitted `KOMM` at 90.
All five proposed words and the `MITTEL` counterexample occur in the existing
hash-pinned 53,465-word lexicon built from ten German books. Their composition
into the clause and the preference for a family message are editorial
judgments. No independent native ciphertext donor or authenticated repeat
of this clause has been identified.

The earlier `DEUTSCHEFELDPOST` corpus match, its discovery and its source
controls remain credited in the [previous edition](p84_field_post_fragment.md).
Those controls do not validate the newly composed clause. No corrected
statistical significance or exhaustive historical uniqueness is claimed.

The new verifier reconstructs the grid, all five source maps, every marginal
letter domain, both word alternatives and each separate repair ledger. It
checks that the three zero-substitution candidates retain all 111 observations,
then re-encrypts complete witnesses under one unchanged global square.
Arbitrary values in unassigned witness slots are not proposed historical
text. Optional PDF verification authenticates the original scan and the
declared crop pixels; no new image asset is required.
