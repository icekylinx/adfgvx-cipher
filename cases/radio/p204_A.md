# p204 A — a radio-station handover and a conditional date digit

[Case guide](../README.md) · [ADFGVX Cipher](../../README.md)

**Status:** The unchanged published `Nov16-18` partial key fixes **69 of 70
plaintext positions** from all **140 valid source coordinates**. The only
unknown is a date digit. A separately recorded calendar inference gives a
plausible new assignment, **FD = 8**, without changing the published key.
**Source review and inference:** icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro. The period key
retains the attribution in [data/keys.json](../../data/keys.json).

## Reading from the published key

> Hiesige Fusta wird laut S.R. Warschau Montag 1? Polen uebergeben.
> Fusta WAUM.

**Meaning:** According to S.R. Warsaw, the local radio station is to be handed
over to Polish control on Monday the 1?. `Fusta` is read as the abbreviated
radio station. `S.R.` is likely the Soldiers' Council in this context, but its
expansion is not part of the decrypted character stream. The raw ending
`FUSTAWAUM` is retained; its final `M` is not silently changed to a separator
or a different call sign.

```text
HIESIGEFUSTAWIRDLAUTXSXRXWARSCHAUMONTAGX1?XPOLENUEBERGEBENXXFUSTAWAUMX
```

Every fixed letter above comes from valid source coordinates and an already
established square cell. No editorial coordinate repair is proposed for this
message.

## Source date

The [source image](../../experiments/open_targets_continuation/sources/childs_p204_selected.png)
is printed p204 / PDF p207, section A. The heading reads **16 November 1918**;
the intercept and internal stamps are:

```text
LP v WAU (3.20 a.m., November 17th)
SN AR KRIEGSMINISTERIUM 2130 (16)
RICHI-140
```

The body preserves every one of the 140 printed coordinates. The date's
unknown unit at plaintext index **41** uses the fully observed digraph
**FD**, square cell **13** in zero-based row-major indexing. Its source
coordinates are indices **92** and **63**. Under the published partial square,
its domain is `03456789`.

## Why 18 November is a strong, conditional reading

The preceding digit `1` and the word `MONTAG` are directly fixed. If the
date refers to **November 1918**, as the message heading and internal stamp
indicate, the compatible candidates are:

| Day in November 1918 | Weekday |
| ---: | --- |
| 10 | Sunday |
| 13 | Wednesday |
| 14 | Thursday |
| 15 | Friday |
| 16 | Saturday |
| 17 | Sunday |
| **18** | **Monday** |
| 19 | Tuesday |

The 11th was also a Monday, but the unknown cell cannot contain `1`: that
digit already occupies another established cell in the injective square.
The 12th is likewise excluded because `2` is already assigned. Thus
**Monday 18 November** is the unique candidate under the stated month,
weekday and square assumptions.

This is a historical inference, not an extra character supplied by the
unchanged key. It assumes that the weekday, month context and two date
coordinates were copied correctly. Source copying errors remain possible.
The repository therefore keeps `FD = 8` in the
[conditional layer](../../experiments/open_targets_continuation/certificates.json),
leaving the published partial square untouched.

## Independent consequences

The same `FD` cell appears in three date fields of the three-part
[p206 A reference list](../military/p201_p206_orders.md). Under the conditional assignment,
all three read **8 November**. This is propagation of one shared key cell,
not three independent confirmations of its value.

None of the six unresolved reference digits in the
[p205 financial](../finance/p205_A.md) and [naval](../naval/p205_B.md) messages uses `FD`.
Consequently, this inference alone supplies **no new fixed p205 character**;
it only removes `8` from the domains of their other unassigned cells.
The separate p201/p206 date-link inference supplies one p205 digit under a
different, explicitly recorded assumption.
