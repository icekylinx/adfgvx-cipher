## Interpretation limits

The damaged source slot belongs to the adjective `NOETIG`; the local fill does not solve a numerical key assignment. The exact output `5UKS`, `NSM` and `6NKJ` is preserved in every layer. In particular, the readily guessed station spelling OSM is not imposed on the received `NSM`. The report establishes only what the sender stated about the connection.
