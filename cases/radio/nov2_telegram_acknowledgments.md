### Earlier-page third-part witness

The p104 source says that parts 1 and 2 are missing and dates its message `1/11`; the three-part copy is dated `2/11`. The p104 body differs from p106 A3 in exactly **source coordinate 54**, V versus X. That changes **plaintext position 68** from 3 to 6: `104359` versus `104659`. Both sources are kept unchanged. The shared wording supports a near-copy relationship, not permission to overwrite either date or reference.
