## Most likely reading

> Wir abbauen 9.11., [05:00 oder 06:00], Weitermarsch Temesvar. Wenn dort aufgebaut,
> rufen wir.

**Display notes:** This readable German includes the two recorded repairs below and keeps both time alternatives. The bracketed whole field is an alternative display of the single unassigned digit in `0?00`; the colon is editorial punctuation. It does not select the later historical `05:00` interpretation.

**Meaning:** The station will be dismantled, followed by an onward march to
Temesvar; it will call again once set up there. The literal cipher-only time is
05:00 or 06:00. Under the already documented historical completion of the
November 7–9 square, it is **05:00 on 9 November**.

The telegram style is retained; *wir bauen ab* is the ordinary German wording.
The proposed month `11` repairs the raw `18`. The proposed `RUFEN` repairs raw
`RUDEN`. Both changes are individually costed below.

[historical square layer](../../experiments/p170_continuation/military_context.json)

additional ciphertext change. The baseline square and its `{5,6}` alternative
remain intact. Accepting the date and verb repairs does not require accepting
