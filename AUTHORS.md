# Contributors

**IceKylin** ([icekylinx](https://github.com/icekylinx)) and **GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI)** jointly conduct and compile this review. GPT-6 Astra is the AI research assistant.

Each message credits its original decipherers and later corrections separately. A successful reproduction is credited as verification, not as the first decipherment.

The historical foundation is J. Rives Childs's collection and the work of wartime cryptanalysts. Modern key recovery and the challenge originate with George Lasry, Ingo Niebel, Nils Kopal, Arno Wacker, and Klaus Schmeh. Individual readings and corrections include contributions by Armin Krauß, Norbert Biermann, Thomas Bosbach, Max Baertl, Prinz with GPT-6 Astra, and Christian Horvat. The catalogue records the applicable source for each contribution.


**Ayush Agarwal (@AyushAg1002)** published the three supplementary body readings in October 2026, with AI assistance as described in his submission. **D. Bourdeau** performed the earlier arithmetic review and maintains the public result index. This edition contributes the later source-image and constraint audit.

The 1934 general-solution paper credits **Frank B. Rowlett, Solomon Kullback, and Abraham Sinkov** for the general methods. **William F. Friedman** edited the historical technical reports; their publication dates are kept separate from the dates of the wartime records.
