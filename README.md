# ADFGVX 1918

A consolidated, reproducible review of the 22 ADFGVX cryptograms published by George Lasry and Klaus Schmeh, with historical sources, prior decipherments, corrections, and new recoveries.

**Research, verification, and compilation:** [IceKylin](https://github.com/icekylinx) and GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI).

Original discoveries remain credited to their original authors. Exact decipherments, source-supported reconstructions, partial readings, and editorial interpretations are recorded separately.

The initial verified findings are being imported in focused commits. The repository will include the complete 22-message catalogue, reviewed readings, executable certificates, and bounded search results.
