# Source-reviewed corpus readings

**ADFGVX Cipher — icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro**

This directory turns selected readable hits from the 788-block OCR retrieval
corpus into image-checked messages, exact coordinate certificates and annotated
German readings. The published period keys remain credited to George Lasry and
the historical and research sources recorded in `data/keys.json`.

The first batch includes [telephone censorship through Budapest](../../cases/other/p85_B.md),
[entry permission for Filittis and family](../../cases/other/p96_B.md), and
[an instruction to use radio for important messages](../../cases/other/p111_B.md).
The first part of the p120–p123 multipart instruction is also source-checked;
the remaining parts are being assembled before its full case page is issued.

## Evidence layers

`selected_sources.json` retains literal rows, the complete header, dates,
printed lengths, source-image coordinates and the selected published key.
Invalid source glyphs are preserved literally and are masked only in the
analytical transcript. A separate field records each same-length editorial
candidate and any geometry changes; those candidates never overwrite the raw
source or the direct reading.

`certificates.json` is regenerated by `verify.py`. The verifier independently
computes column offsets and plaintext-to-source maps, compares the result with
the project decoder, reconstructs every source coordinate, and re-encrypts
every complete editorial candidate with both implementations. Known-coordinate
substitutions, erasure fills, deletions and unchanged coordinates are counted
separately. Orthographic insertions appearing only in the readable prose are
explicitly outside a same-length certificate.

```sh
python3 experiments/corpus_readings/verify.py --verify
# Optional: also check the exact PDF used for visual inspection.
python3 experiments/corpus_readings/verify.py --verify --pdf /path/to/adfgvx-childs.pdf
```

OCR likelihood scores were used only to select promising pages for inspection.
They establish neither a historical letter nor first-decipherment priority.
The committed excerpts and literal transcripts are the evidence for the
selected readings. This batch is not a claim that the entire source corpus has
been deciphered or that every OCR block represents a separate message.
