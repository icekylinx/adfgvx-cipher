# Surviving parts of an eight-part Transylvania situation report

Prepared by **icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro**.

This package gives exact source editions for **five readable surviving parts: 1, 4, 5, 7 and 8**. It also preserves the unread fragment of part 6 separately. Parts 2 and 3 have not been located. The assembly is a proposed association of physical sources on Childs pp128, 129 and 137, supported by their matching `1840 / 18.40`, `8 TLE` and `LP v NKJ` fields, their part numbers, the published Nov4–6 key, and coherent military-withdrawal content. The inconsistent page/receipt dates and times remain explicit.

The first source page itself says that parts 2–6 are missing. That statement is accurate for its copy and is preserved in the source crop. A separate page supplies part 4, an interference-damaged part 5 and a short surviving fragment of part 6. These are not invented continuations or an exact duplicated-plaintext witness.

For the declared candidate, the minimum is three changes to observed coordinates. The five-coordinate gap is at source boundary **69**, between the second and third printed rows. The single-coordinate insertion ties at **5, 6, 7 or 8**. Each inserts A into the existing run of As, making `AAAAV`; presentation uses index 8, before the V. All four source ownership maps and raw readings are retained, even though their completed ciphertext is identical. The five-coordinate fill is `GVGVA`.

The primary candidate changes source 19 A→X, 46 G→F and 100 X→G; fills printed N and C with V; and fills the six unprinted coordinates. These are exact reversible edits. The fit is conditional on the explicit candidate and the one-plus-five loss family; it is not an independent original-text witness.

## Part 5: explicit interference, a conditional partial reading

The source advertises 252 coordinates. It contains **215 literal slots**, including **four printed dashes**, and two explicit `INTERFERENCE` annotations at source boundaries 190 and 215. Therefore, if the advertised length is accepted, the two unprinted runs total **37 coordinates**. The four dashes are already occupied source slots and are not added to that deficit.

The verifier retains **all 38 nonnegative allocations**, from 0/37 through 37/0. There is no automatic equal split or assignment based on the width of the printed annotation. Across all 38 models, 64 plaintext positions have the same singleton value; the complete union of possible domains is preserved.

The optional 20-character lexical crib `ANSCHLIESSENDMUESSEN`, at position zero, is compatible with all observed coordinates only for allocation **0/37**. Every other allocation needs at least one observed-coordinate contradiction in that crib. This is a **conditional choice**: it assumes that the prefix is the intended wording and was copied correctly at its observed coordinates. It does not prove that the first interference interval had zero lost symbols. The first marker can describe reception trouble without a net missing coordinate only under this model; all other allocations remain available.

## Independent archival context

A [compact archival locator](archival_context.json) identifies the original Groener telegram of 4 November (Ia Nr. 11212 Geh.Op.), Lersner’s Nr. 2733, and Horstmann’s Nr. 579 on NARA T136 reel 39. ## Bounded copy search and remaining gaps

All six surviving physical sources, including the unread part 6, are searched against all **788** archived OCR blocks. The method compares exact ten-coordinate ADFGVX windows within each contiguous printed span, excludes only the listed self-blocks, and never crosses an `INTERFERENCE` annotation or invalid glyph. All hits are retained.

The only nonself result is three overlapping windows, a twelve-coordinate stretch, between part 8 and `p146_ocr_block4`. This short match is not certified as a duplicate and supplies no missing part. The other five queries have no nonself exact ten-coordinate match in this bounded corpus. This does not exclude a corrupted, differently keyed, differently divided or untranscribed copy. Parts 2 and 3 remain unlocated; they cannot be directly queried without an independent fragment.
