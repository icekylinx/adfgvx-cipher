# p73 archival retrieval route

**ADFGVX Cipher · icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro · 2 November 2026**

This is a catalogue-access follow-up to the
[existing uninspected archival leads](../../data/reviews/p73_source_context.json),
not a new telegram, date correction or plaintext proposal.

The German Federal Foreign Office's
[official digitization inventory](https://archiv.diplo.de/arc-de/digitalisierte-und-einsehbare-bestaende-invenio--2404424)
lists **RZ 201 / Abteilung IA as completely digitized**, available through its
invenio application. Its
[official research and citation guide](https://archiv.diplo.de/arc-de/im-archiv-forschen/bestellen-und-zitieren-mit-invenio)
explains that old central-office `R` item numbers survive under newer `RZ`
record-group references, and recommends searching `RZ*` followed by the old
number in the signature field.

For the literature leads already retained here, the concrete catalogue
queries are therefore **`RZ*11061`** and **`RZ*11064`**. These are search
instructions derived from the official guide, not newly verified item
identities. The individual titles, date ranges and relevant telegram pages
still need direct inspection. In particular, a citation to an 26 October
reply does not establish its German wording, 09:11 timestamp, encrypted length
or identity with p73.

The official guide describes a separate Political Archives account, rather
than a Bundesarchiv-wide account. On this review's public text-fetch attempt,
the linked [invenio entry](https://politisches-archiv.diplo.de/invenio/login.xhtml)
timed out and its linked help page returned a gateway error. No individual
catalogue record or document image was retrieved in this follow-up. The
collection-level digitization statement must not be promoted into evidence
that a particular message has been inspected or matched.

The original [bounded source review](README.md) remains unchanged. Both date
layers and all p73 ciphertext coordinates are preserved.
