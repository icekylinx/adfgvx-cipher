## Interpretation limits

The matching calendar dates in an unrelated 1917 editorial passage must not be
treated as evidence that p73 used the Julian calendar. The
[existing calendar hypothesis](../../data/reviews/p73_source_context.json)
already records the arithmetic correspondence and the countervailing Georgian
calendar-reform evidence. This follow-up adds no basis for choosing one date
layer over the other.

Similarly, a dated letter by the same general cannot determine a cipher text's
subject or encryption date. The relevant result here is the inspected scope and
the lack of a matching witness within it. An unpublished, unavailable or
unindexed parallel remains possible. The [p73 case](../../cases/pages/073.md#edition-cases-p73) remains
open.
