# Optional Nov22–24 interpretation: GD may stand for J

Prepared by **icekylinx and GPT-6 Astra Pro**.

Two separate language-based routes support interpreting coordinate pair **GD** as **J** under the published Nov22–24 ranks. The current baseline square labels that cell **9**. This package records the inference as an optional partial-square constraint, propagates every consequence in a declared source scope, and preserves all original keys, ciphertexts and source repair costs.

The result is not a newly recovered historical digit. It makes five letter positions conditional J in the 21 reviewed physical records, and reduces 77 other primary-source position domains without fixing those positions. The exact p215 reference count changes from **90 to 72** under the already published optional numerical assumptions. The additional all-digits subset stays **42**.

## Two distinct reasons to test J

### Earlier literal NIKOLAJEW and later NIKOLA9EW

The source edition of **p173 A1**, under the Nov10–12 key, directly reads `NIKOLAJEW` at plaintext positions 60–68. All eighteen coordinates of the name are observed, and none changes in its earlier ordinary-word edition. This package independently reconstructs those positions and their source coordinates.

Three later physical parts read `NIKOLA9EW`: p212 A1, p213 A6 and p213 A7. The name's nine letters are unchanged by the earlier source repairs. In p212 A1 the reading also survives all four tied missing-group geometries.

**Additional assumption:** these later passages use the same correctly copied place-name spelling as the earlier literal `NIKOLAJEW`. Under that assumption, their GD cell has value J. The ciphertext alone does not establish the identity of the place or guarantee a correctly copied name. The three later parts belong to related medical traffic; they are not three independent historical identifications.

### A separate ordinary word: 9EWEILS

The newly source-edited **p216 B2** directly reads:

```text
HERMANNSTADTXSENDENXANKUNFTAUFDEN8STATIONENOKM9EWEILSMITTEILENXKROGEHENHIERZUENDEXOKMXYAYY?18XX
```

Its `9EWEILS` is in a request to notify arrival at stations. Reading it as the ordinary German word `JEWEILS` supplies a second, distinct lexical route. Plaintext position 46 is GD from original source coordinates 116=`G` and 185=`D`; both are printed and unrepaired. This observation does not depend on the p216 first part's conditional gap reconstruction or on identifying a place or person.

The ciphertext proves the pair GD. The language assumption supplies J. These are deliberately separate claims.

## A further adverse name occurrence: NIKOLA3EW

The newly source-edited p216 A2 gives another name-like field, **NIKOLA3EW**, at plaintext positions 46–54. Its position 52 is **FD** from printed source 26=`F`, 180=`D`; all name coordinates are observed and untouched by the ordinary-word edition. It is included in the inventory even though its raw label is 3 rather than 9.

Under the earlier numerical layer, this FD cell could still be J. **After assigning J to GD, injectivity excludes J at FD.** The unchanged source therefore cannot be completed to `NIKOLAJEW` under the new hypothesis. A universal assumption that every occurrence of the apparent name was correctly copied would be too strong.

Changing source 26 F to G would change FD to GD and allow that spelling at the cost of one additional source-coordinate substitution. The certificate reproduces that calculation as an explicitly **unapplied** counterfactual; it does not repair the source, count another recovered letter, or include the hypothetical frame among the 54 variants. This adverse occurrence remains visible alongside the three supportive `NIKOLA9EW` fields and independent `9EWEILS` word. The conditional J inference is useful without pretending that it removes copying uncertainty.

## Partial squares and the separate numerical assumptions

All unproved baseline labels `0123456789JQY` are first cleared. A new interpretation is one injective partial square, with one global pool of unused symbols. Domains at separate plaintext positions cannot be selected independently with repetition.

| Layer | Assumptions | Remaining unassigned symbols |
|---|---|---|
| Lexical J alone | raw 9→J | `0123456789QY` |
| Earlier numerical layer | raw Y→1, raw J→2, raw 2→6 | `0345789JQY` |
| Explicit conjunction | Earlier numerical layer plus raw 9→J | `0345789QY` |

The earlier numerical assignments come from [operational_recovery](../operational_recovery/README.md) and the separately declared historical-formation context. They are not derived from `NIKOLAJEW` or `JEWEILS`. Conversely, either lexical route can be examined without accepting those numerical assignments.

Every variant is independently decrypted under all three partial squares. The certificate contains complete position domains, every domain that changes when J is added to the earlier numerical layer, and one globally injective full-square/erasure witness for each reading. Such a witness proves consistency with the declared constraints; it is an arbitrary completion, not recovered historical text.

Across the 21 primary raw frames, 82 positions change domains: the five GD positions become J and 77 other domains shrink. No source coordinate is changed. Many raw ordinary-word defects and numerical fields remain unresolved or still look wrong. Earlier ordinary-word candidate variants are listed separately so their source edits are not attributed to the new constraint.

## Exact p215 footer propagation: 90 → 72, and 42 → 42

The preserved Norbert two-gap geometry yields the four observed footer cells `FD FA XD AV`, baseline `3Y52`. Under the earlier numerical assumptions their values are:

```text
a1b6
```

The cells for a and b are distinct and still unassigned. Before GD=J, each draws without replacement from the ten-symbol pool `0345789JQY`: **10 × 9 = 90** literal values. After assigning J to GD, neither footer cell may also be J. Their pool is `0345789QY`: **9 × 8 = 72** values. The 18 excluded values are exactly those with J in either unresolved position.

If one additionally requires all four footer characters to be digits, the remaining digit pool is `0345789` both before and after: **7 × 6 = 42** values. Leading zero is allowed; no numeral is chosen. The all-digit requirement remains an extra assumption about the field.

This is a full enumeration of 90 and 72 footer values, not an independent per-position estimate. For every value the verifier constructs an injective completion of the entire square, fills only the already erased coordinates, decrypts the full message, and re-encrypts it to the same source-preserving ciphertext. The certificate retains all witnesses. The p215 gap placements and every observed coordinate stay unchanged.

Baertl's separate 130-character prose completion is retained as prior attribution but is not relabeled as a source-compatible variant of the 129-position frame. Its inherited best single-deletion comparisons already require at least 27 contradictions to observed coordinates. This package does not turn that wording into a new certified model.
